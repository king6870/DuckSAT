Step-by-Step Explanation:

Therefore, $$[\triangle ADE] = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot \frac{16}{3} = 2 \cdot \frac{16}{3} = \frac{32}{3}.$$ Thus the correct answer is choice $A$ and the area is $\frac{32}{3}$.
