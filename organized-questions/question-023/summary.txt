In right triangle ABC with A at (0,0), B at (6,0), and C at (0,4), point D lies on AB with AD = 2. A line through D is drawn parallel to BC and intersects AC at E. What is the area of triangle ADE?

ANSWER CHOICES:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) 4/3
B) 2
C) 8/3
D) 4

CORRECT ANSWER:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) $\\frac{4}{3}$

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: In triangle $ABC$ with $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, $C=(0,4)$, note that $AB$ lies on the $x$-axis and $AC$ lies on the $y$-axis. Since $AD=2$, the point $D$ on $AB$ is $D=(2,0)$.
Step 2: The slope of $BC$ is $m_{BC}=\frac{4-0}{0-6}=-\frac{2}{3}$.
Step 3: A line through $D$ parallel to $BC$ has the same slope, so its equation is $y = m_{BC}(x-2) = -\frac{2}{3}(x-2)$.
Step 4: Intersect this line with $AC$ (which is the line $x=0$). At $x=0$, $y = -\frac{2}{3}(-2) = \frac{4}{3}$, hence $E=(0,\frac{4}{3})$.
Step 5: The triangle $ADE$ has vertices $A=(0,0)$, $D=(2,0)$, $E=(0,\frac{4}{3})$, so its legs along the $x$- and $y$-axes have lengths $AD=2$ and $AE=\frac{4}{3}$, and the angle at $A$ is a right angle.
Step 6: The area is $[ADE]=\tfrac{1}{2}\,AD\,AE=\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 2\cdot \tfrac{4}{3}=\tfrac{4}{3}$.
Therefore the correct choice is $A$ and the area is $\tfrac{4}{3}$.
