Step 6: The area is $[ADE]=\tfrac{1}{2}\,AD\,AE=\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 2\cdot \tfrac{4}{3}=\tfrac{4}{3}$. Therefore the correct choice is $A$ and the area is $\tfrac{4}{3}$.
