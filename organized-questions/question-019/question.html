Answer: B) 12

Step-by-Step Explanation:

Let the rectangle have lower-left corner at $ (0,0) $ and its opposite corner at $ (x,y) $ on the hypotenuse. The hypotenuse is the line through $ (8,0) $ and $ (0,6) $, which has slope $ m = \frac{6-0}{0-8} = -\frac{3}{4} $ and equation $ y = -\frac{3}{4}x + 6 $. Since the rectangle is axis-aligned, its area is $ A = x y $. Substituting $ y = 6 - \frac{3}{4}x $ gives $ A(x) = x \left(6 - \frac{3}{4}x\right) = 6x - \frac{3}{4}x^2 $. Rewrite as a completed square: $ A(x) = -\frac{3}{4}x^2 + 6x = -\frac{3}{4}\left(x^2 - 8x\right) = -\frac{3}{4}\left[(x-4)^2 - 16\right] = -\frac{3}{4}(x-4)^2 + 12 $. Therefore the maximum occurs when $(x-4)^2 = 0$, i.e., $ x = 4 $, giving $ A = 12 $. The corresponding $ y $-coordinate is $ y = 6 - \frac{3}{4}(4) = 3 $, so the rectangle has dimensions $ x = 4 $ and $ y = 3 $. Thus the maximum possible area is $ A = 12 $, which is choice B.
