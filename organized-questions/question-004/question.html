💡 Solution

Answer: A) 6

Step-by-Step Explanation:

Step 1: The circle with center at the origin and radius 5 has equation $x^2 + y^2 = 25$. Step 2: The vertical line is $x = 4$, which is at distance $d = 4$ from the center. Step 3: Intersecting the line with the circle: $4^2 + y^2 = 25 \Rightarrow y^2 = 9$, so the intersection points are $P(4,3)$ and $Q(4,-3)$. Step 4: The chord length $PQ$ is the distance between these points: $$|PQ| = \sqrt{(4-4)^2 + (3-(-3))^2} = \sqrt{0 + 36} = 6.$$ Alternatively, using the chord-length formula for a circle of radius $R$ cut by a line at distance $d$ from the center, $L = 2\sqrt{R^2 - d^2}$, with $R = 5$ and $d = 4$, we get $$L = 2\sqrt{25 - 16} = 2\sqrt{9} = 6.$$ Therefore, the correct choice is A.
