In the coordinate plane, let A = (0,0), B = (8,0), and C = (0,6). A line through C with slope -4/3 intersects segment AB at D. What is the area of triangle BCD?

----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) 10.5
B) 12
C) 15
D) 21

CORRECT ANSWER:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) $\\frac{21}{2}$

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Let the given points be $A = (0,0)$, $B = (8,0)$, and $C = (0,6)$. A line through $C$ with slope $-\\frac{4}{3}$ has equation $y-6 = -\\frac{4}{3}(x-0)$, i.e. $y = 6 - \\frac{4}{3}x$. The segment $AB$ lies on the line $y=0$, so the intersection point $D$ satisfies $0 = 6 - \\frac{4}{3}x$, giving $x = \\frac{9}{2}$. Thus $D = (\\frac{9}{2}, 0)$ and $D$ lies on $AB$ since $0 \\le \\frac{9}{2} \\le 8$.

The base of triangle $BCD$ on the $x$-axis is $BD = |8 - \\frac{9}{2}| = \\frac{7}{2}$. The height with respect to this base is the perpendicular distance from $C$ to the line $y=0$, which is $|6| = 6$.

Hence the area is 
$\\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot BD \cdot \text{height} = \\tfrac{1}{2} \cdot \\frac{7}{2} \\cdot 6 = \\tfrac{21}{2} = 10.5$.

Therefore, the correct answer is choice A.
