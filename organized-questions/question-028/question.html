Answer: A) $12$

Step-by-Step Explanation:

Since \(\angle B\) is the right angle, \(AB\) and \(BC\) are the legs of triangle \(ABC\). Compute their lengths from the coordinates: \\(AB = \sqrt{(-4 - 0)^2 + (0 - 0)^2} = \sqrt{(-4)^2 + 0^2} = \sqrt{16} = 4.\\ BC = \sqrt{(0 - 0)^2 + (0 - 6)^2} = \sqrt{0^2 + (-6)^2} = \sqrt{36} = 6.\\ For a right triangle, the area is \(A = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot AB \cdot BC\). Thus \\(A = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot 6 = 12.\\ Thus the correct choice is \(A\) and the area is \(12\).
