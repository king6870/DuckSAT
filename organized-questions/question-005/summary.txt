In the coordinate plane, a right triangle is drawn with the right angle at A. A square is placed in the corner at A with sides along the legs of the triangle, and the far vertex of the square lies on the hypotenuse. Given B(8,0) and C(0,6) are the other two vertices of the triangle, what is the area of the inscribed square?

A) 576/49
B) 64/7
C) 36
D) 144/7

CORRECT ANSWER:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) $\frac{576}{49}$

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------

3) Solve for s: $$s + \frac{3}{4}s = 6 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{7}{4}s = 6 \quad\Rightarrow\quad s = \frac{24}{7}.$$
4) The area of the inscribed square is A_{square} = s^2 = \left(\frac{24}{7}\right)^2 = \frac{576}{49}.$$ 
5) Therefore, the correct answer is choice A, and the area is $$\frac{576}{49}.$$
