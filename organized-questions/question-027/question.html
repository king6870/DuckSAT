💡 Solution

Answer: B) $3:2$

Step-by-Step Explanation:

Step 1: In a trapezoid with $AB \parallel CD$, the diagonals intersect at $E$ and satisfy a proportionality between the segments: $\frac{AE}{CE} = \frac{AB}{CD}$. Justification (via similarity): Consider triangles $AEB$ and $CED$. Since $AB \parallel CD$, corresponding angles formed with line $BE$ are equal, and the angle at $E$ is common; hence triangles $AEB$ and $CED$ are similar by AA. Therefore $\frac{AE}{CE} = \frac{AB}{CD}$. Step 2: Compute base lengths: $AB = 9$, $CD = 6$. Step 3: Hence $\frac{AE}{EC} = \frac{AB}{CD} = \frac{9}{6} = \frac{3}{2}$, so $AE:EC = 3:2$. Step 4: Coordinate check (optional): Parametrize $AC$ by $A + t(C-A) = (6t, 4t)$, $0 \le t \le 1$. Parametrize $BD$ by $B + s(D-B) = (9-9s, 4s)$, $0 \le s \le 1$. Set equal: $6t = 9-9s$ and $4t = 4s$. From $4t = 4s$, we have $t = s$. Substituting gives $6t = 9 - 9t$, so $15t = 9$, hence $t = \frac{3}{5}$. On $AC$, $AE:EC = t:(1-t) = \frac{3}{5}:\frac{2}{5} = 3:2$.
