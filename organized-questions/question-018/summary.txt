Based on the bar chart showing the number of tickets sold for four showings labeled A, B, C, and D, what is the probability that a randomly selected ticket came from showing D?

CORRECT ANSWER:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) $\frac{5}{14}$

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Let $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ denote the number of tickets sold for showings $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ respectively. The total number of tickets sold is $T = a + b + c + d$. The probability that a randomly selected ticket came from showing $D$ is $P(D) = \frac{d}{T}$. Read the bar chart to obtain $d$ and $T$. For this chart, $d = 5$ and $T = 14$, so $P(D) = \frac{5}{14}$. Since $\frac{5}{14}$ corresponds to choice $A$, the correct answer is $A$.
