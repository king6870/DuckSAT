In triangle ABC, AB = AC = 6 and BC = 8. An altitude AD is drawn to BC. What is the measure of angle ABC?

💡 Solution

Answer: A) $48.2^\\circ$

Step-by-Step Explanation:

Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$ to $BC$. Since $AB = AC$, triangle $ABC$ is isosceles with base $BC$, so $D$ is the midpoint of $BC$. Thus $BD = DC = \frac{BC}{2} = \frac{8}{2} = 4$. In right triangle $ABD$ (right angle at $D$), $AB^{2} = AD^{2} + BD^{2}$, so $AD^{2} = AB^{2} - BD^{2} = 6^{2} - 4^{2} = 36 - 16 = 20$, hence $AD = \sqrt{20} = 2\sqrt{5}$. In triangle $ABD$, angle $ABC$ corresponds to angle $ABD$, and $\tan(\angle ABC) = \frac{AD}{BD} = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$. Therefore $\angle ABC = \arctan\left(\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\right) \approx 48.2^{\circ}$. Thus the correct choice is $A$.
