In triangle ABC, angle ABC is a right angle. The legs AB and BC have lengths 6 and 8, respectively, and the hypotenuse AC has length 10. A perpendicular from B to AC meets AC at D. What is the length of BD?

----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) 4.5
B) 4.8
C) 5.0
D) 4.2

CORRECT ANSWER:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
B) $4.8$

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Note the right triangle with ∠ABC = 90°, and sides $AB = 6$, $BC = 8$, and $AC = 10$. Step 2: Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $B$ to the hypotenuse $AC$, so $BD$ is the altitude to the hypotenuse.

Step 3: Use the area of triangle $ABC$ in two ways. First, using the legs: $$[ABC] = \frac{1}{2} \cdot AB \cdot BC = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 8 = 24.$$ Second, using the hypotenuse and altitude: $$[ABC] = \frac{1}{2} \cdot AC \cdot BD = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot BD = 5\cdot BD.$$ Step 4: Equate the two expressions for the area: $$5\cdot BD = 24 \quad\Rightarrow\quad BD = \frac{24}{5} = 4.8.$$

Result: The length of $BD$ is $\frac{AB \cdot BC}{AC} = \frac{6 \cdot 8}{10} = \frac{24}{5} = 4.8$, which corresponds to choice $\textbf{B}$.
