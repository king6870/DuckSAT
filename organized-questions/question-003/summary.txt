----------------------------------------------------------------------

$$L = 2\sqrt{R^2 - d^2}.$$

$$6 = 2\sqrt{5^2 - d^2} \quad\Rightarrow\quad 3 = \sqrt{25 - d^2}.$$

$$9 = 25 - d^2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad d^2 = 16 \quad\Rightarrow\quad d = 4.$$
