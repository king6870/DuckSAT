💡 Solution

Answer: C) 4

Step-by-Step Explanation:

Step 1: Identify the circle's radius. Since AB is a diameter of length 10, the radius is $R = \frac{10}{2} = 5$. Step 2: Let the center of the circle be $O$, and let $d$ be the perpendicular distance from $O$ to the chord $CD$. Because $AB$ and $CD$ are parallel, the distance between the lines $AB$ and $CD$ equals $d$. Step 3: Use the chord-length relation. For a chord of length $L$ at a distance $d$ from the center of a circle of radius $R$, we have $$L = 2\sqrt{R^2 - d^2}.$$ With $L = 6$ and $R = 5$, this gives $$6 = 2\sqrt{5^2 - d^2} \quad\Rightarrow\quad 3 = \sqrt{25 - d^2}.$$ Squaring both sides yields $$9 = 25 - d^2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad d^2 = 16 \quad\Rightarrow\quad d = 4.$$ Step 4: The distance between the parallel lines $AB$ and $CD$ is $d = 4$. Answer: The distance is $4$, so the correct choice is C.
