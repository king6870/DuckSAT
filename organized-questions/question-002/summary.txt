In the coordinate plane, right triangle ABC has a right angle at A with AB = 8 and AC = 6. A square A D F E is drawn with vertex A at the right angle, with AD along AB, AE along AC, and the opposite vertex F on BC. What is the area of square ADEF?

CORRECT ANSWER:

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The opposite vertex F is at (s, s).\nThus, the square sits with sides parallel to the coordinate axes.\n\nStep 3: Since F lies on BC, the point (s, s) must satisfy the equation of line BC.
