Answer: A) $\frac{576}{49}$

Step-by-Step Explanation:

Step 1: Place the right triangle on a coordinate plane. Let A = (0,0), AB lie along the x-axis with B = (8,0), and AC lie along the y-axis with C = (0,6). Then BC is the line from (8,0) to (0,6).\n\nStep 2: The square ADEF has A at the origin, AD along AB, and AE along AC. If the side length of the square is s, then\n- D is at (s, 0) on AB, and\n- E is at (0, s) on AC. The opposite vertex F is at (s, s).\nThus, the square sits with sides parallel to the coordinate axes.\n\nStep 3: Since F lies on BC, the point (s, s) must satisfy the equation of line BC. The line BC passes through (8,0) and (0,6), so its slope is m = (6-0)/(0-8) = -3/4, and its equation is\n\[ y = -\frac{3}{4}x + 6 \] or equivalently \(3x + 4y = 24\).\nSubstituting F(s, s) gives\n\[ 3s + 4s = 24 \quad \Rightarrow \quad 7s = 24 \quad \Rightarrow \quad s = \frac{24}{7}. \]\\n\\nStep 4: The area of square ADEF is s^2 = (24/7)^2 = 576/49.\n\nAnswer: A, with area $\frac{576}{49}$.
