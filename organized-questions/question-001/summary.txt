QUESTION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
In the right triangle ABC, a right angle is at A. Given AB = 6 and AC = 8, let D be the foot of the perpendicular from A to BC. What is the length of AD?

ANSWER CHOICES:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) 4.0
B) 4.8
C) 6.0
D) 9.6

CORRECT ANSWER:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
B) 4.8

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
In right triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at $A$, we have $AB = 6$ and $AC = 8$. The hypotenuse $BC$ is
$$BC = \sqrt{AB^2 + AC^2} = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{100} = 10.$$

Let $AD$ be the altitude from $A$ to the hypotenuse $BC$. The area of triangle $ABC$ can be computed two ways:
1) Using legs: $[ABC] = \frac{1}{2} AB \cdot AC = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 8 = 24.$
2) Using base and altitude: $[ABC] = \frac{1}{2} BC \cdot AD = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot AD = 5AD.$
Equating the two expressions for the area gives $24 = 5AD$, so $AD = \frac{24}{5} = 4.8.$

Therefore, the length of $AD$ is $4.8$, which corresponds to choice B. (An alternative formula is $AD = \dfrac{AB \cdot AC}{BC} = \dfrac{6 \cdot 8}{10} = 4.8$.)
