In the diagram, triangle ABC is a right triangle with a right angle at B. The coordinates are A(-4,0), B(0,0), and C(0,6). A bar chart beside the triangle displays the lengths of sides AB, BC, and AC. Based on the diagram, what is the area of triangle ABC?

D) 24

CORRECT ANSWER:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) $12$

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Since \(\angle B\) is the right angle, \(AB\) and \(BC\) are the legs of triangle \(ABC\). Compute their lengths from the coordinates: \\(AB = \sqrt{(-4 - 0)^2 + (0 - 0)^2} = \sqrt{(-4)^2 + 0^2} = \sqrt{16} = 4.\\
BC = \sqrt{(0 - 0)^2 + (0 - 6)^2} = \sqrt{0^2 + (-6)^2} = \sqrt{36} = 6.\\
For a right triangle, the area is \(A = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot AB \cdot BC\). Thus \\(A = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot 6 = 12.\\
Thus the correct choice is \(A\) and the area is \(12\).
