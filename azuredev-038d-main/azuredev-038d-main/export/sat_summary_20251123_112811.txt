QUESTION:

In triangle ABC, points A, B, and C have coordinates A(0,0), B(8,0), and C(3,6). Point D lies on AB such that AD:DB = 1:3. What is the length of segment CD?

ANSWER CHOICES:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) sqrt(29)
B) sqrt(37)
C) sqrt(40)
D) sqrt(45)

CORRECT ANSWER:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
B) $\sqrt{37}$

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Find the coordinates of $D$ on the segment $AB$ given the ratio $AD:DB = 1:3$. For internal division, with $A=(0,0)$, $B=(8,0)$, and ratio $m:n$ where $m=1$, $n=3$, the coordinates are
$$D = \left( \frac{n x_A + m x_B}{m+n}, \frac{n y_A + m y_B}{m+n} \right) = \left( \frac{3\cdot 0 + 1\cdot 8}{4}, \frac{3\cdot 0 + 1\cdot 0}{4} \right) = (2,0).$$
So $D=(2,0)$.

Alternatively, since $AB$ lies on the $x$-axis and $AD:DB=1:3$, $AD = \frac{1}{1+3} \cdot AB = \frac{1}{4} \cdot 8 = 2$, giving the same $D=(2,0)$.

Step 2: Compute the length of $CD$ using the distance formula. With $C=(3,6)$ and $D=(2,0)$,
$$CD = \sqrt{(x_C - x_D)^2 + (y_C - y_D)^2} = \sqrt{(3-2)^2 + (6-0)^2} = \sqrt{1^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{37}.$$ 

Therefore, the length of $CD$ is $\sqrt{37}$, which corresponds to option $\text{B}$.
