QUESTION:

In the coordinate plane, triangle ABC has vertices A(0,0), B(6,0), and C(0,8). Point D lies on AB with AD = 4. A line through D is drawn parallel to BC and intersects AC at E. What is the area of triangle ADE?

ANSWER CHOICES:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) 32/3
B) 16
C) 24
D) 8

CORRECT ANSWER:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A) $\\frac{32}{3}$

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Let $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, $C=(0,8)$. Since $AD=4$ on segment $AB$ and $AB$ lies along the $x$-axis from $(0,0)$ to $(6,0)$, we have $D=(4,0)$. The slope of $BC$ is
$$m_{BC}=\frac{8-0}{0-6}= -\frac{4}{3}.$$
A line through $D$ parallel to $BC$ has slope $m=-\frac{4}{3}$ and equation
$$y-0 = m(x-4) \quad\Rightarrow\quad y = -\frac{4}{3}(x-4) = -\frac{4}{3}x + \frac{16}{3}.$$
The line $AC$ is the segment from $(0,0)$ to $(0,8)$, i.e. the line $x=0$. Intersecting with $x=0$ gives $E=(0,\frac{16}{3})$. Thus the triangle $ADE$ has vertices $A=(0,0)$, $D=(4,0)$, $E=(0,\frac{16}{3})$. Since $AD$ lies on the $x$-axis and $AE$ lies on the $y$-axis, angle $A$ is a right angle, so
$$[\triangle ADE] = \frac{1}{2}\,|AD|\,|AE|.$$
Here $|AD|=4$ and $|AE|=\left|\frac{16}{3}-0\right|=\frac{16}{3}$. Therefore,
$$[\triangle ADE] = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot \frac{16}{3} = 2 \cdot \frac{16}{3} = \frac{32}{3}.$$
Thus the correct answer is choice $A$ and the area is $\frac{32}{3}$.
