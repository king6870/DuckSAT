In the coordinate plane, triangle ABC is right-angled at A with A(0,0), B(6,0), and C(0,8). A square is drawn with vertex at A and sides parallel to the axes, such that the opposite vertex lies on BC. What is the area of the square?

EXPLANATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The line $BC$ has slope $m = \\frac{8-0}{0-6} = -\\frac{4}{3}$, and using point $B$, its equation is $y - 0 = -\\frac{4}{3}(x - 6)$, so $y = -\\frac{4}{3}x + 8$. A square with a vertex at $A$ and sides parallel to the axes has vertices at $A = (0,0)$, $(s,0)$, $(0,s)$, and the opposite vertex at $(s,s)$. Since the opposite vertex lies on $BC$, the point $(s,s)$ satisfies the line equation: $s = -\\frac{4}{3}s + 8$. Solving: $s + \\\frac{4}{3}s = 8 \\Rightarrow \\\frac{7}{3}s = 8 \\\Rightarrow s = \\\frac{24}{7}$. The area is $s^{2} = \\\left(\\frac{24}{7}\\right)^{2} = \\\frac{576}{49}$. Therefore the correct choice is $A$.
