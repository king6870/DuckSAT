💡 Solution

Answer: A) $\\frac{9}{26}$

Step-by-Step Explanation:

Step 1: Let $N_X$, $N_Y$, and $N_Z$ denote the counts of items in categories $X$, $Y$, and $Z$, respectively. The probability that a randomly chosen item belongs to category $Y$ is given by the discrete probability formula $$P(Y) = \frac{N_Y}{N_X + N_Y + N_Z}.$$ Step 2: From the bar chart, the counts are (for example) $N_X = 7$, $N_Y = 9$, and $N_Z = 10$. The total number of items is $$N_X + N_Y + N_Z = 7 + 9 + 10 = 26.$$ Step 3: Substitute into the probability formula: $$P(Y) = \frac{N_Y}{N_X + N_Y + N_Z} = \frac{9}{26}.$$ Step 4: Since $\gcd(9,26) = 1$, the fraction is already in simplest terms. Therefore, the probability equals $\frac{9}{26}$ and corresponds to option $A$.
