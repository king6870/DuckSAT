Question 1 EASY
Factor and solve: Solve the quadratic equation $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$ by factoring. What are the solutions?
  • A) $x = 2$ or $x = 3$
  • B) $x = -2$ or $x = -3$
  • C) $x = 1$ or $x = 6$
  • D) $x = 5$ or $x = -1$
Explanation:
Factor the quadratic: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x - 2)(x - 3)$. By the zero-product property, $(x - 2)=0$ or $(x - 3)=0$, so $x = 2$ or $x = 3$.
Question 2 EASY
A rectangle has a fixed perimeter of 20 meters. Let the width be $x$ meters and the length be $10 - x$ meters, so the area is $A(x) = x(10 - x) = -x^2 + 10x$. What value of $x$ maximizes the area?
  • A) 2
  • B) 5
  • C) 10
  • D) 0
Explanation:
Let $A(x) = -x^2 + 10x$. Completing the square: $A(x) = -\bigl(x^2 - 10x\bigr) = -\bigl((x - 5)^2 - 25\bigr) = -(x - 5)^2 + 25$. This is a downward-opening parabola with vertex at $(5,25)$, so the maximum area occurs when $x=5$. The maximum area is $A(5) = 25$ square meters.

  • A) $x = 2$ or $x = 3$
  • B) $x = -2$ or $x = -3$
  • C) $x = 1$ or $x = 6$
  • D) No real solutions
Explanation:
Step 1: Factor the quadratic: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3)$.\
Step 2: Apply zero-product property: set each factor to zero: $x-2=0$ or $x-3=0$.\
Step 3: Solve: $x=2$ or $x=3$.\
Therefore, the solutions are $x=2$ and $x=3$.
Question 4 MEDIUM
Rewrite $y = 3x^2 + 12x - 7$ in vertex form $y = a(x-h)^2 + k$. Which option is the correct vertex form?
  • A) $y = 3(x + 2)^2 - 19$
  • B) $y = 3(x - 2)^2 - 19$
  • C) $y = 3(x + 2)^2 + 19$
  • D) $y = -3(x + 2)^2 - 19$
Explanation:
Step 1: Factor out 3: $y = 3(x^2 + 4x) - 7$.\
Step 2: Complete the square inside: $x^2 + 4x + 4 = (x+2)^2$, so add and subtract 4 inside the bracket: $y = 3[(x+2)^2 - 4] - 7$.\
Step 3: Distribute and simplify: $y = 3(x+2)^2 - 12 - 7 = 3(x+2)^2 - 19$.\
Thus the vertex form is $y = 3(x+2)^2 - 19$, which corresponds to vertex $(-2,-19)$.

  • A) 0
  • B) 1
  • C) 2
  • D) Infinitely many
Explanation:
Step 1: For $P(x)=0$, identify $a=-2$, $b=30$, $c=-40$.\
Step 2: Compute the discriminant: $D = b^2 - 4ac = 30^2 - 4(-2)(-40) = 900 - 320 = 580$.\
Step 3: Since $D > 0$, there are two distinct real solutions to $P(x) = 0$.\
Conclusion: There are 2 real break-even points.
