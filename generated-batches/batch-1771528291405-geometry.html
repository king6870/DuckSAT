Question 1 EASY
In the right triangle $\triangle ABC$ with right angle at $C$, $AB = 10$, and $\angle A = 30°$, what is the length of $\overline{AC}$?
  • A) $5$
  • B) $5\sqrt{3}$
  • C) $5\sqrt{2}$
  • D) $10$
Explanation:
Because $\triangle ABC$ is right and $AB$ is the hypotenuse, $\cos A = \frac{\overline{AC}}{AB}$. With $A=30°$ and $AB=10$, $\cos 30° = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, so $\overline{AC} = AB \cdot \cos A = 10 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 5\sqrt{3}$. Therefore, the correct choice is B.
Question 2 EASY
In the coordinate plane, points $A(1,2)$ and $B(4,6)$ are given. What is the length of segment $\overline{AB}$?
  • A) $5$
  • B) $3\sqrt{2}$
  • C) $\sqrt{13}$
  • D) $6$
Explanation:
Use the distance formula: $AB = \sqrt{(x_B - x_A)^2 + (y_B - y_A)^2} = \sqrt{(4-1)^2 + (6-2)^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9+16} = \sqrt{25} = 5$.
Question 3 EASY
In a circle with center $O$ and radius $5$, the central angle $\angle AOB$ measures $60°$. What is the length of arc $AB$?
  • A) $\frac{5\pi}{3}$
  • B) $\frac{5\pi}{6}$
  • C) $\frac{10\pi}{3}$
  • D) $\pi$
Explanation:
Arc length for a central angle is $s = r\theta$ with $\theta$ in radians. Here $\theta = 60° = \pi/3$ and $r = 5$. Hence $s = 5 \cdot (\pi/3) = \frac{5\pi}{3}$. So A is correct.
Question 4 MEDIUM
In $\triangle ABC$, with $\angle C = 90°$, $\overline{AB} = 10$, and $\angle A = 30°$, what is the area of $\triangle ABC$?
  • A) $\frac{25\sqrt{3}}{2}$
  • B) $25\sqrt{3}$
  • C) $50$
  • D) $\frac{25}{2}$
Explanation:
Step 1: Since $\angle C = 90°$, $AB$ is the hypotenuse. With $\angle A = 30°$, the other angles are $60°$ and $30°$; the side opposite $30°$ is $BC$ and the side adjacent to $A$ is $AC$.\ Step 2: $BC = AB\sin A = 10 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = 5$, and $AC = AB\cos A = 10 \cdot \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 5\sqrt{3}$.\ Step 3: Area = $\tfrac{1}{2} \cdot BC \cdot AC = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 5\sqrt{3} = \tfrac{25\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
Question 5 MEDIUM
Let $P(2,-1)$ and $Q(-4,5)$ lie on line $L$. What is the distance from the origin to line $L$?
  • A) $\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
  • B) $\tfrac{1}{2}$
  • C) 1
  • D) $\sqrt{2}$
Explanation:
Step 1: Slope of $PQ$ is $m = \dfrac{5-(-1)}{-4-2} = \dfrac{6}{-6} = -1$, so the line has equation $y+1 = -1(x-2)$, i.e. $x+y-1=0$.\ Step 2: The distance from the origin to the line $ax+by+c=0$ is $\dfrac{|c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$ with $a=1$, $b=1$, $c=-1$; thus distance = $\dfrac{| -1|}{\sqrt{1^2+1^2}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.

Question 7 MEDIUM
In $\triangle ABC$ with $\overline{AB}=6$, $\overline{BC}=8$, $\overline{CA}=10$, what is the length of the altitude from $B$ to $\overline{AC}$?
  • A) 4.8
  • B) 3.2
  • C) 6
  • D) 8
Explanation:
Step 1: Check Pythagoras: $AB^2 + BC^2 = CA^2$ ( $6^2 + 8^2 = 10^2$ ), so $\angle B$ is a right angle and $CA$ is the hypotenuse. Step 2: The altitude from $B$ to $AC$ (the hypotenuse) in a right triangle is given by $h = \dfrac{AB\cdot BC}{AC}$. Thus $h = \dfrac{6\cdot 8}{10} = \dfrac{48}{10} = 4.8$.
