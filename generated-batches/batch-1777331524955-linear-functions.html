Question 1 EASY
A car rental company charges a base fee of 25 dollars plus 30 dollars per day of rental. Let $x$ be the number of days rented. Which equation gives the total cost $C$ in dollars as a function of $x$?
  • A) $C = 25 + 30x$
  • B) $C = 30x - 25$
  • C) $C = 25x + 30$
  • D) $C = x/30 + 25$
Explanation:
Step 1: The base fee is paid regardless of days, so when $x=0$, the cost is $C=25$. Step 2: For each additional day, cost increases by $30$, so the total cost is $C = 25 + 30x$. Step 3: This matches the slope-intercept form $C = mx + b$ with $m=30$ (rate per day) and $b=25$ (base). Step 4: The other options are incorrect: B subtracts the base fee, C multiplies days by 25 instead of 30, and D uses division by 30.
Question 2 EASY
Which of the following equations represents a line perpendicular to the line $y = -2x + 5$?
  • A) $y = -2x + 1$
  • B) $y = 0.5x - 3$
  • C) $y = -0.5x + 4$
  • D) $y = x + 5$
Explanation:
Step 1: The given line has slope $m_1 = -2$. Step 2: A perpendicular line has slope $m_2 = -\frac{1}{m_1}$. With $m_1 = -2$, we get $m_2 = -\frac{1}{-2} = 0.5$ (or $0.5$). Step 3: Among the options, only option B has slope $0.5$, so it is perpendicular. Step 4: Check other options: A has slope $-2$ (parallel), C has slope $-0.5$; $(-2)(-0.5) = 1
eq -1$, so not perpendicular, D has slope $1$; $(-2)(1) = -2
eq -1$.
Question 3 MEDIUM
A car rental company charges a flat fee of 40 dollars plus 0.25 dollars per mile. Let y be the total cost in dollars and x be miles driven. Which equation in slope-intercept form correctly represents the total cost?
  • A) y = 0.25x + 40
  • B) y = 40x + 0.25
  • C) y = 0.25x - 40
  • D) y = -0.25x + 40
Explanation:
Step 1: The per-mile charge is the coefficient of $x$, so $m = 0.25$. Step 2: The flat fee is the y-intercept, $b = 40$. Step 3: Put together in slope-intercept form: $y = mx + b = 0.25x + 40$. Therefore option A is correct. The other options misuse the intercept or signs: (B) multiplies the fee by miles, (C) subtracts the flat fee, (D) makes the cost decrease with miles.
Question 4 MEDIUM
Which equation represents a line perpendicular to the line given by $y = 4x + 1$?
  • A) $y = -1/4 x + 2$
  • B) $y = 1/4 x + 2$
  • C) $y = -4x + 2$
  • D) $y = 4x - 2$
Explanation:
The slope of the given line is $m = 4$. A line perpendicular has slope equal to the negative reciprocal, which is $-1/4$. Among the options, only (A) has slope $-1/4$, so it is correct. The other options have incorrect slopes: (B) uses the positive reciprocal, (C) uses the reciprocal rather than negative reciprocal, (D) keeps the original slope (parallel).
Question 5 MEDIUM
The line passes through (0,6) and (3,0). What is the slope m, and what does this slope represent in context?
  • A) The slope is -2, meaning y decreases by 2 for every 1 unit increase in x.
  • B) The slope is -2, meaning y increases by 2 for every 1 unit increase in x.
  • C) The slope is 2, meaning y increases by 2 for every 1 unit increase in x.
  • D) The slope is 0, meaning y does not change with x.
Explanation:
Step 1: Compute the slope: $m = \frac{0-6}{3-0} = \frac{-6}{3} = -2$. Step 2: The slope $-2$ means that for each 1 unit increase in $x$, $y$ changes by $-2$ units (i.e., decreases by 2). Step 3: Therefore the statement in option A is correct. Options B, C misstate the sign, the magnitude, or the concept of a zero slope.
