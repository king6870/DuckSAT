Question 19 HARD
In a small theater, the price of an adult ticket is 2 dollars more than a student ticket. If 3 adult tickets and 2 student tickets are sold for a total of 16 dollars, what are the prices of the adult and student tickets (in dollars)?
  • A) Adult 5 dollars, Student 3 dollars
  • B) Adult 6 dollars, Student 4 dollars
  • C) Adult 4 dollars, Student 2 dollars
  • D) Adult 3 dollars, Student 1 dollar
Explanation:
Let x be the adult price and y be the student price. We have x = y + 2 and 3x + 2y = 16. Substitute x = y + 2 into the second equation: 3(y + 2) + 2y = 16 => 3y + 6 + 2y = 16 => 5y = 10 => y = 2. Then x = y + 2 = 4. So adult = 4 dollars, student = 2 dollars.
