Question 18 HARD
A bag contains 4 red balls, 3 blue balls, and 5 green balls. Three balls are drawn without replacement. What is the probability that at least one red ball is drawn?
  • A) 41/55
  • B) 3/5
  • C) 9/11
  • D) 2/3
Explanation:
Total ways to draw 3 balls from 12 is $\binom{12}{3} = 220$. The number of ways to draw no red ball is choosing all 3 from the 8 non red balls (3 blue and 5 green): $\binom{8}{3} = 56$. So the probability of at least one red is $1 - \frac{56}{220} = \frac{164}{220} = \frac{41}{55}$. Therefore the correct option is A. Step by step: compute total combinations, compute no red combinations, subtract from 1.
