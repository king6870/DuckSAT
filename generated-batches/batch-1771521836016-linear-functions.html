Question 1 EASY
A car rental company charges a base daily fee of 25 dollars plus 0.50 dollars per mile driven. Let y be the total cost in dollars and x be the miles driven. Which equation in slope-intercept form expresses y in terms of x?
  • A) y = 25x + 0.50
  • B) y = 0.50x + 25
  • C) y = 25x - 0.50
  • D) y = -0.50x + 25
Explanation:
In slope-intercept form, $y = mx + b$, where $m$ is the slope (cost per mile) and $b$ is the intercept (base daily fee). Here the slope $m = 0.50$ and the intercept $b = 25$, so $y = 0.50x + 25$. The other options swap the intercept and slope or use the wrong sign.
Question 2 EASY
A line passes through (2, 3) and has slope m = 4. Write the equation in slope-intercept form.
  • A) y = 4x + 3
  • B) y = 4x - 5
  • C) y = -4x + 3
  • D) y = x - 2
Explanation:
Use the point-slope form with (x1, y1) = (2, 3): $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1) \Rightarrow y - 3 = 4(x - 2) \Rightarrow y - 3 = 4x - 8 \Rightarrow y = 4x - 5$.
Question 3 EASY
Which equation represents a line perpendicular to $y = -\frac{1}{3}x + 4$?
  • A) y = 3x - 2
  • B) y = -3x + 5
  • C) y = $\frac{1}{3}x$ + 4
  • D) y = -x + 4
Explanation:
The slope of the given line is $m = -\frac{1}{3}$. A line perpendicular has slope equal to the negative reciprocal: $m_{\perp} = -\frac{1}{m} = -\frac{1}{-\frac{1}{3}} = 3$. Among the options, only A has slope 3, so it is perpendicular.

Question 8 MEDIUM
Two lines have slopes $m_1 = 2$ and $m_2 = -\frac{1}{2}$. Which statement is true?
  • A) They are parallel.
  • B) They are perpendicular.
  • C) They are neither parallel nor perpendicular.
  • D) They are the same line.
Explanation:
Two lines are parallel if their slopes are equal; here $2 eq -\frac{1}{2}$, so not parallel. They are perpendicular if $m_1 m_2 = -1$, and $2 \cdot (-\frac{1}{2}) = -1$, so the lines are perpendicular. Therefore B is correct.

Question 18 HARD
Using the point (2, -3) on a line with slope m = -4, convert from the point-slope form to slope-intercept form. Which equation is the slope-intercept form?
  • A) $y = -4x + 5$
  • B) $y = -4x - 3$
  • C) $y = 4x + 5$
  • D) $y = -4x + 3$
Explanation:
Start from the point-slope form y − y1 = m(x − x1) with y1 = −3, x1 = 2, m = −4. Then y − (−3) = −4(x − 2) → y + 3 = −4x + 8 → y = −4x + 5. Therefore, option A is correct. Option B would come from improperly removing the +3 term; option C uses the opposite slope; option D uses the wrong intercept.
Question 19 HARD
Line L1 has slope m1 = 3/4. Find the equation of line L2 that passes through (6, −2) and is perpendicular to L1. Express your answer in slope-intercept form.
  • A) $y = -\frac{4}{3}x + 6$
  • B) $y = \frac{4}{3}x + 6$
  • C) $y = -\frac{3}{4}x + 6$
  • D) $y = -\frac{4}{3}x - 6$
Explanation:
A line perpendicular to a line with slope m1 has slope m2 = −1/m1. Here m1 = 3/4, so m2 = −4/3. Through (6, −2): y − (−2) = (−4/3)(x − 6) → y + 2 = (−4/3)x + 8 → y = (−4/3)x + 6. Thus option A is correct. B uses the positive reciprocal, which is not perpendicular. C uses the wrong reciprocal sign and magnitude. D has the correct slope but the intercept is incorrect given the point.
