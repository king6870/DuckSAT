Question 1 EASY
Solve by factoring: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$.
  • A) $x = 2$ or $x = 3$
  • B) $x = 1$ or $x = 6$
  • C) $x = 0$ or $x = 5$
  • D) $x = -2$ or $x = -3$
Explanation:
Factor the quadratic: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3)$. By the zero-product property, set each factor equal to zero: $x-2=0$ or $x-3=0$. So $x=2$ or $x=3$.

  • C) $x = 1$ or $x = -2$
  • D) $x = 0$ or $x = -2$
Explanation:
Here $a=2$, $b=3$, $c=-2$. Discriminant: $D=b^2-4ac=9-4(2)(-2)=9+16=25$. $x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{D}}{2a}=\frac{-3\\pm5}{4}$. Thus $x=\frac{2}{4}=\frac{1}{2}$ or $x=\frac{-8}{4}=-2$.

A ball is thrown upward from height 2 feet with initial velocity 48 ft/s. Height is modeled by $h(t) = -16t^2 + 48t + 2$. After how many seconds does the ball hit the ground?
  • A) 3.04 s
  • B) 2.00 s
  • C) 4.80 s
  • D) 1.50 s
Explanation:
Set $h(t)=0$: $-16t^2+48t+2=0$. Multiply by $-1$: $16t^2-48t-2=0$. Divide by 2: $8t^2-24t-1=0$. Use quadratic formula: $t=\frac{24\pm\sqrt{(-24)^2-4(8)(-1)}}{2\\cdot8}=\frac{24\pm\sqrt{576+32}}{16}=\frac{24\pm\sqrt{608}}{16}=\frac{24\\pm4\sqrt{38}}{16}=\frac{6\pm\sqrt{38}}{4}$. Positive root is $\frac{6+\sqrt{38}}{4}\approx 3.04$ seconds.
