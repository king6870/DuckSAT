Question 1 EASY
A population starts at $800$ and doubles every $2$ years. What is the population after $6$ years?
  • A) 3200
  • B) 4800
  • C) 6400
  • D) 12800
Explanation:
There are 3 doubling periods in 6 years since $6 \div 2 = 3$. The population after 6 years is $P = 800 \times 2^3 = 800 \times 8 = 6400$.

Question 6 EASY
Compute $\log_5(125) - \log_5(25)$.
  • A) 0
  • B) 1
  • C) 2
  • D) 3
Explanation:
Use the log difference identity: $\log_b(a) - \log_b(c) = \log_b\left(\dfrac{a}{c}\right)$. So $\log_5(125) - \log_5(25) = \log_5\left(\dfrac{125}{25}\right) = \log_5(5) = 1$.

Question 12 MEDIUM
A sample of radioactive material has a half-life of 6 hours. If the initial mass is 200 grams, how many grams remain after 18 hours?
  • A) 12.5
  • B) 25
  • C) 50
  • D) 100
Explanation:
After each half-life, mass halves. In 18 hours there are $18/6=3$ half-lives: $200 \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^3 = 200/8 = 25$ grams.

Question 15 MEDIUM
Solve $\log_2(x) + \log_2(x-1) = 3$. Find the valid value(s) of x.
  • A) $(1+\sqrt{33})/2$
  • B) $(1-\sqrt{33})/2$
  • C) 2
  • D) 4
Explanation:
Combine logs: $\log_2(x(x-1)) = 3$ ⇒ $x(x-1)=8$ ⇒ $x^2 - x - 8 = 0$ ⇒ $x = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{33}}{2}$. Domain requires $x>1$, so $x = \frac{1+\sqrt{33}}{2}$ is the valid value. Numerically ≈ 3.37.

  • A) $3 \log_2(10)$
  • B) 9
  • C) $3 \log_2(100)$
  • D) $\dfrac{9}{2}$
Explanation:
Let $P_0 = 5000$ and $P(t) = 5000 \cdot 2^{t/3}$. To reach 50,000, solve $5000 \cdot 2^{t/3} = 50{,}000$. This gives $2^{t/3} = 10$. Taking logarithms base 2 yields $t/3 = \log_2(10)$, so $t = 3 \log_2(10)$. This is the first time the population reaches 50,000 since the model doubles every 3 years. Numerically, $t \approx 9.97$ years.
Question 18 HARD
Solve for $x > 1$: $\log_2(x-1) + \log_2(x+1) = 2$, using logarithm properties.
  • A) $\sqrt{5}$
  • B) $-\sqrt{5}$
  • C) $\dfrac{5}{2}$
  • D) 3
Explanation:
Combine logs: $\log_2(x-1) + \log_2(x+1) = \log_2((x-1)(x+1)) = 2$. So $\log_2(x^2-1) = 2$, hence $x^2 - 1 = 4$ and $x^2 = 5$. Since the domain requires $x > 1$, the valid solution is $x = \sqrt{5}$.

Question 20 HARD
Compute the value of $(1 + i)^8$ where $i^2 = -1$.
  • A) 16
  • B) -16
  • C) 16i
  • D) -8 + 8i
Explanation:
Use successively: $(1+i)^2 = 1 + 2i + i^2 = 2i$. Then $(1+i)^4 = (2i)^2 = -4$, and $(1+i)^8 = (-4)^2 = 16$. Hence the result is real and positive.
