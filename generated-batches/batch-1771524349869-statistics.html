  • A) 80

  • C) 85
  • D) 90

  • D) Not enough information
Explanation:

Step 4: Add the new observation 11. New sum: $118 + 11 = 129$. New count: $20 + 1 = 21$. Step 5: New mean: $\frac{129}{21} = \frac{43}{7} \approx 6.142857$. To two decimals, this is $6.14$. Therefore the new mean is 6.14.
Question 18 HARD
A bag contains red, blue, and green balls: 3 red, 2 blue, and 5 green (total 10 balls). Three balls are drawn without replacement. What is the probability that at least one red ball is drawn?
  • A) 7/24
  • B) 17/24
  • C) 3/10
  • D) 13/24
Explanation:
Step 1: Total ways to draw 3 balls from 10: $\binom{10}{3} = 120$. Step 2: No red drawn means all 3 come from the 7 non-red balls (2 blue + 5 green): $\binom{7}{3} = 35$. Step 3: Probability of no red = $\frac{35}{120} = \frac{7}{24}$. Step 4: Probability of at least one red = $1 - \frac{7}{24} = \frac{17}{24}$. Answer: $\frac{17}{24}$.

Question 20 HARD
The quarterly revenues (in millions) are: Q1 = 120, Q2 = 150, Q3 = 180, Q4 = 210. These values show a steady upward trend. What is the standard deviation (sample) of these four values, to the nearest tenth?
  • A) 38.7
  • B) 33.5
  • C) 28.3
  • D) 41.5
Explanation:
Step 1: Compute the mean: $\bar{x} = \frac{120+150+180+210}{4} = \frac{660}{4} = 165$. Step 2: Deviations: $120-165=-45$, $150-165=-15$, $180-165=15$, $210-165=45$; squared deviations: $2025, 225, 225, 2025$; sum = 4500. Step 3: Sample variance: $s^2 = \frac{\sum (x_i-\bar{x})^2}{n-1} = \frac{4500}{3} = 1500$. Step 4: Sample standard deviation: $s = \sqrt{1500} \approx 38.7$. Answer: approximately 38.7.
