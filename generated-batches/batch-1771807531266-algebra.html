Question 64 HARD
Compute the product: $ (x^2 - 1)(x^2 - 9) $.
  • A) $x^4 - 10x^2 + 9$
  • B) $x^4 - 8x^2 - 9$
  • C) $x^4 - 10x^2 - 9$
  • D) $x^4 + 10x^2 + 9$
Explanation:
Use difference of squares: $(x^2 - 1)(x^2 - 9) = (x^2 - 1)(x^2 - 9) = x^4 - 9x^2 - x^2 + 9 = x^4 - 10x^2 + 9$.
Question 65 HARD

  • A) $2x + 1$ with remainder $6$
  • B) $2x + 1$ with remainder $-6$
  • C) $2x - 1$ with remainder $6$
  • D) $2x + 2$ with remainder $6$
Explanation:
Polynomial division gives quotient $2x + 1$ and remainder $6$, so $2x^2 - 3x + 4 = (x - 2)(2x + 1) + 6$.
