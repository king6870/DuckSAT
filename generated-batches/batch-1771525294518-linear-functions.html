Question 1 EASY
A taxi charges $2 per mile plus a base fee of $3. The total fare $y$ (in dollars) for $x$ miles is modeled by $y = 2x + 3$. Which statement is true about the slope and $y$-intercept?
  • A) Slope is 2 and y-intercept is 3.
  • B) Slope is -2 and y-intercept is -3.
  • C) Slope is 1/2 and y-intercept is 3.
  • D) Slope is -1/2 and y-intercept is -3.
Explanation:
In the slope-intercept form $y = mx + b$, $m$ represents the slope and $b$ the $y$-intercept. For the equation $y = 2x + 3$, the slope is $m = 2$ and the $y$-intercept is $b = 3$. Therefore, statement A is true and the others are not.
Question 2 EASY
A line passes through $(2, 3)$ with slope $4$. Write its equation in slope-intercept form.
  • A) $y = 4x - 5$
  • B) $y = 4x + 5$
  • C) $y = -4x + 5$
  • D) $y = -4x - 5$
Explanation:
Using the point-slope form $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ with $(x_1, y_1) = (2, 3)$ and $m = 4$, we get $y - 3 = 4(x - 2)$. This simplifies to $y = 4x - 5$, which matches option A.

Question 18 HARD
A line with slope $m = -\frac{2}{3}$ passes through $(6,-5)$. Which of the following is its equation in slope-intercept form?

  • B) $y = -\frac{2}{3}x + 7$
  • C) $y = \frac{2}{3}x - 1$
  • D) $y = -\frac{3}{2}x - 5$
Explanation:

Therefore option A is correct. Option B has the wrong intercept; C uses the wrong sign for the slope; D uses the reciprocal slope and an incorrect constant term.
