Solve for x: $2(x - 3) + 4 = 5x - 7$.
  • A) $x = \frac{5}{3}$
  • B) $x = -\frac{5}{3}$
  • C) $x = 2$
  • D) $x = \frac{7}{3}$
Explanation:
Step 1: Expand left side: $2(x - 3) + 4 = 2x - 6 + 4 = 2x - 2$. Step 2: Set equal to right side: $2x - 2 = 5x - 7$. Step 3: Subtract $2x$ from both sides: $-2 = 3x - 7$. Step 4: Add 7 to both sides: $5 = 3x$. Step 5: Divide by 3: $x = \frac{5}{3}$.

A chemist mixes two liquids, x liters of liquid A and y liters of liquid B. Twice the amount of A plus the amount of B equals 7 liters, and A is 1 liter more than B. Solve for x and y.
  • A) $(x, y) = (\frac{8}{3}, \frac{5}{3})$
  • B) $(x, y) = (1, 0)$
  • C) $(x, y) = (0, 7)$
  • D) $(x, y) = (-1, 3)$
Explanation:
From $2x + y = 7$ and $x - y = 1$, solve: from $x - y = 1$ we get $y = x - 1$. Substitute into $2x + (x - 1) = 7$: $3x - 1 = 7 \Rightarrow 3x = 8$ so $x = \frac{8}{3}$ and $y = x - 1 = \frac{5}{3}$.
