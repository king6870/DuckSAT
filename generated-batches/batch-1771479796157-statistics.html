  • B) 2/7
  • C) 6/7
  • D) 4/7

  • D) The mean increases and the median remains 7.

Step 1: Compute the initial mean and median. Sum = 3+5+5+7+8+9+12 = 49, so mean = 49/7 = 7. The sorted list is 3,5,5,7,8,9,12, so the median (middle value) is 7. Step 3: The new sorted list is 3,5,5,7,8,9,12,100; the median (average of 4th and 5th values) is $(7+8)/2 = 7.5$. Step 4: Therefore, statement B is true while A, C, and D are false.
Question 18 HARD
In a survey of 200 people, 120 prefer coffee, 95 prefer tea, and 40 prefer both. If a person is chosen at random, what is the probability that the person prefers coffee or tea (or both)?
  • A) $\frac{7}{8}$
  • B) $\frac{5}{8}$
  • C) $\frac{3}{8}$
  • D) $\frac{11}{20}$
Explanation:
Step 1: P(coffee) = 120/200 = 0.60; P(tea) = 95/200 = 0.475; P(both) = 40/200 = 0.20. Step 2: P(coffee or tea) = P(coffee) + P(tea) - P(both) = (120+95-40)/200 = 175/200 = 7/8. Step 3: Therefore, the probability is $\frac{7}{8}$.

  • B) s
  • C) 2s
