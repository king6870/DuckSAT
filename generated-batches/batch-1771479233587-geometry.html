In right triangle $\triangle ABC$ with right angle at $C$, $AC = 3$ and $BC = 4$. What is the length of the hypotenuse $AB$?
  • A) 4
  • B) 5
  • C) 7
  • D) 3
Explanation:

Question 2 EASY

  • A) $\frac{25\pi}{6}$
  • B) $\frac{25\pi}{3}$
  • C) $\frac{5\pi}{3}$
  • D) $\frac{25\pi}{12}$
Explanation:
Area of a sector with central angle $\theta$ is $A = \frac{\theta}{360} \cdot \pi r^2$. Here $\theta = 60°$, $r = 5$: $A = \frac{60}{360} \cdot \pi \cdot 25 = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 25\pi = \frac{25\pi}{6}$.

Explanation:
Step 1: Note that in triangle ACD, the sides AD and AC form the included angle ∠DAC equal to ∠A = 60°. Step 2: Apply the Law of Cosines: $CD^2 = AD^2 + AC^2 - 2\cdot AD\cdot AC\cdot \cos 60°$. Substitute AD=3, AC=6, cos60°=1/2: $CD^2 = 3^2 + 6^2 - 2\cdot 3\cdot 6\cdot \frac{1}{2} = 9 + 36 - 18 = 27$. Step 3: Therefore $CD = \sqrt{27} = 3\sqrt{3}$.
