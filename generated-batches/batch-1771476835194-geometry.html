Question 1 EASY
In coordinate geometry, let $A(1, 2)$ and $B(4, 6)$. What is the length of $\overline{AB}$?
  • A) 3
  • B) 4
  • C) 5
  • D) 7
Explanation:
Use the distance formula for the length of segment $\overline{AB}$: $AB = \sqrt{(x_B - x_A)^2 + (y_B - y_A)^2}$. Here $A = (1,2)$ and $B = (4,6)$, so $AB = \sqrt{(4-1)^2 + (6-2)^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9+16} = \sqrt{25} = 5$.
Question 2 MEDIUM
In the coordinate plane, points $P(2,3)$ and $Q(7,-1)$ are given. The distance $PQ$ equals which of the following?
  • A) $\sqrt{41}$
  • B) $\sqrt{50}$
  • C) $\sqrt{34}$
  • D) $\sqrt{32}$
Explanation:
Distance formula: $PQ = \sqrt{(x_Q - x_P)^2 + (y_Q - y_P)^2} = \sqrt{(7-2)^2 + (-1-3)^2} = \sqrt{5^2 + (-4)^2} = \sqrt{25+16} = \sqrt{41}$.
