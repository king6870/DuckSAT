Question 1 EASY
Factor and solve the quadratic equation $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$ by factoring.
  • A) $x = 2$ or $x = 3$
  • B) $x = -2$ or $x = -3$
  • C) $x = 1$ or $x = 6$
  • D) $x = 0$ or $x = 5$
Explanation:
Step 1: Identify $a=1$, $b=-5$, $c=6$. Step 2: Factor the quadratic: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3)$. Step 3: Apply zero product property: $(x-2)=0$ or $(x-3)=0$, giving $x=2$ or $x=3$. Therefore, the solutions are $x=2$ and $x=3$.

A projectile's height after $t$ seconds is $h(t) = -5t^2 + 40t$ meters. Use completing the square to rewrite $h(t)$ in vertex form and then determine the times when the height is zero, i.e., when $h(t)=0$.
  • A) $t=0$ or $t=8$
  • B) $t=4$ only
  • C) $t=-8$ or $t=0$
  • D) $t=0$ only
Explanation:
Step 1: Complete the square: $h(t) = -5t^2 + 40t = -5(t^2 - 8t) = -5[(t-4)^2 - 16] = -5(t-4)^2 + 80$. Step 2: Set $h(t)=0$: $-5(t-4)^2 + 80 = 0 \\Rightarrow (t-4)^2 = 16$. So $t-4 = \pm 4$, giving $t = 0$ or $t = 8$. Hence the projectile is at ground level at $t=0$ and $t=8$ seconds.

Which width maximizes area, and what is the maximum area?
  • A) $w = 15$, $A = 450$
  • B) $w = 20$, $A = 400$
  • C) $w = 18$, $A = 432$
  • D) $w = 9$, $A = 378$
Explanation:
Express $l$ as $l = 60 - 2w$, then $A$ = $w l$ = $w(60 - 2w) = -2w^2 + 60w$. The vertex occurs at $w = -\dfrac{b}{2a} = -\dfrac{60}{2(-2)} = 15$. Thus the maximum area is $A(15) = -2(15)^2 + 60(15) = -450 + 900 = 450$. So $w = 15$ maximizes area with $A_{\\max} = 450$.

Question 10 HARD
Let $h(t) = -2t^2 + 8t + 5$ be the height (in meters) of a ball $t$ seconds after it is thrown upward. What is the maximum height reached by the ball?
  • A) 13 meters
  • B) 11 meters
  • C) 9 meters
  • D) 7 meters
Explanation:
Step 1: Complete the square to rewrite in vertex form: $h(t) = -2t^2 + 8t + 5 = -2(t^2 - 4t) + 5$. Step 2: Complete the square inside: $t^2 - 4t = (t - 2)^2 - 4$. Step 3: Substitute back: $h(t) = -2[(t - 2)^2 - 4] + 5 = -2(t - 2)^2 + 13$. Step 4: The vertex is at $(h, k) = (2, 13)$, and since $a = -2 < 0$, the maximum height occurs at $t = 2$ and equals $13$ meters.
