  • A) $x = \frac{8}{3}, \; y = \frac{5}{3}$
  • B) $x = 4, \; y = 3$
  • C) $x = -\frac{8}{3}, \; y = -\frac{5}{3}$
  • D) $x = 3, \; y = 2$
Explanation:
Add the two equations to eliminate y: $(2x + y) + (x - y) = 7 + 1$ gives $3x = 8$, so $x = \frac{8}{3}$. Substitute into $x - y = 1$: $\frac{8}{3} - y = 1$ so $y = \frac{5}{3}$.

Question 9 HARD
A theater sells adult tickets for 5 dollars and child tickets for 3 dollars. On a certain day, x adult tickets and y child tickets were sold, for a total of 7 tickets and total revenue of 31 dollars. How many adult tickets were sold? (Set up and solve the system: $5x + 3y = 31$, $x + y = 7$.)
  • A) $3$
  • B) $5$
  • C) $2$
  • D) $7$
Explanation:
Let x be the number of adult tickets and y the number of child tickets. From the total tickets: $x + y = 7$ ⇒ $y = 7 - x$. Substitute into the revenue equation: $5x + 3(7 - x) = 31$ ⇒ $5x + 21 - 3x = 31$ ⇒ $2x = 10$ ⇒ $x = 5$. Therefore, 5 adult tickets were sold.
Question 10 HARD
Factor the polynomial $4x^3 - 4x^2 - 9x + 9$.
  • A) $\,(x - 1)(2x - 3)(2x + 3)$
  • B) $\,(x - 5)(2x - 3)(2x + 3)$
  • C) $\,(x + 1)(2x - 3)(2x + 3)$
  • D) $(x - 1)(4x^2 - 9)$
Explanation:
Group terms: $4x^3 - 4x^2 - 9x + 9 = 4x^2(x - 1) - 9(x - 1) = (x - 1)(4x^2 - 9) = (x - 1)(2x - 3)(2x + 3)$. This matches option A, which is the correct factorization.
