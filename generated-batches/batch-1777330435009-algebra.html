Question 2 EASY
In a two-number problem, the sum of the two numbers is 7 and the larger number exceeds the smaller by 3. If $x$ denotes the larger number and $y$ the smaller, what is the value of $x$? (Use the system $x+y=7$, $x-y=3$.)
  • A) 2
  • B) 3
  • C) 4
  • D) 5
Explanation:
Step-by-step: From the system $x+y=7$ and $x-y=3$, add the equations: $(x+y)+(x-y)=7+3$ which gives $2x=10$, hence $x=5$. Then substitute into $x+y=7$ to get $5+y=7$, so $y=2$. Therefore the larger number is $x=5$.
Question 3 MEDIUM
A number $x$ increased by 7 is equal to twice the number plus 3. Solve for $x$.
  • A) 7
  • B) 4
  • C) 3
  • D) 5
Explanation:
From $x + 7 = 2x + 3$, subtract $x$ from both sides to get $7 = x + 3$. Subtract $3$ from both sides to obtain $4 = x$. Therefore, $x = 4$.
Question 4 MEDIUM
Solve the absolute value inequality $|2x - 3| \\le 5$ and express the solution as an interval.
  • A) [-1, 4]
  • B) (-1, 4)
  • C) [-1, 3]
  • D) (-$\infty$, -1] $\cup$ [4, $\infty)$
Explanation:
From $|2x - 3| \\le 5$ we have -5 ≤ 2x - 3 ≤ 5. Adding 3 gives -2 ≤ 2x ≤ 8. Dividing by 2 yields -1 ≤ x ≤ 4. The solution interval is [-1, 4].
Question 5 MEDIUM
In a fruit stand, the price of an apple is a dollars and the price of a banana is b dollars. The stand sells 3 apples and 2 bananas for 15 dollars, and 1 apple and 4 bananas for 9 dollars. Find a and b.
  • A) a = $\frac{21}{5}$, b = $\frac{6}{5}$
  • B) a = $\frac{9}{2}$, b = $\frac{3}{2}$
  • C) a = 5, b = 1
  • D) a = 3, b = 2
Explanation:
Let a be the price of an apple and b the price of a banana. From the purchases: $3a + 2b = 15$ and $a + 4b = 9$. From the second equation, $a = 9 - 4b$. Substitute into the first: $3(9 - 4b) + 2b = 15 \Rightarrow 27 - 12b + 2b = 15 \Rightarrow -10b = -12$, so $b = \frac{6}{5}$. Then $a = 9 - 4(\frac{6}{5}) = 9 - \frac{24}{5} = \frac{21}{5}$. Therefore, $a = \frac{21}{5}$ and $b = \frac{6}{5}$.
