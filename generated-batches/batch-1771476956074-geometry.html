Question 1 EASY
In triangle $\triangle ABC$ with coordinates $A(0,0)$, $B(4,0)$, and $C(0,3)$, what is the area of $\triangle ABC$?
  • A) 4
  • B) 12
  • C) 6
  • D) 9
Explanation:
The triangle is right at A because AB is along the x-axis and AC is along the y-axis. Base AB = 4 and height AC = 3. Therefore, area = $\frac{1}{2} \cdot AB \cdot AC = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot 3 = 6$. Using the determinant formula also yields $\frac{1}{2} | x_1(y_2-y_3) + x_2(y_3-y_1) + x_3(y_1-y_2) | = \frac{1}{2} | 0(0-3) + 4(3-0) + 0(0-0) | = 6$.
Question 2 MEDIUM
Let $P(1,2)$ and $Q(5,6)$ lie on line $\\ell$. The line $\\ell$ intersects the x-axis at $R$. What is the distance $QR$?
  • A) $4\sqrt{2}$
  • B) $6\sqrt{2}$
  • C) 12
  • D) 6
Explanation:
Step 1: The slope of $PQ$ is $m = \frac{6-2}{5-1} = 1$. Step 2: The line through $P$ with slope 1 is $y-2 = 1(x-1)$, i.e. $y = x+1$. Step 3: Intersect with x-axis ($y=0$): $0 = x+1 \\Rightarrow x = -1$, so $R(-1,0)$. Step 4: The distance $QR$ is $\sqrt{(5-(-1))^2 + (6-0)^2} = \sqrt{6^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{72} = 6\sqrt{2}$.
