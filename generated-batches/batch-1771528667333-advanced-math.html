Question 1 EASY
The population of a town doubles every $5$ years. If the current population is $8{,}000$, what will be the population after $15$ years?
  • A) 32{,}000
  • B) 64{,}000
  • C) 96{,}000
  • D) 128{,}000
Explanation:
Step 1: Doubling every 5 years means P = P0 · 2^{t/5}. Here P0 = 8{,}000 and t = 15, so exponent = 15/5 = 3. Step 2: Compute P = 8{,}000 · 2^{3} = 8{,}000 · 8 = 64{,}000. Therefore the answer is B. $P = 8{,}000 \cdot 2^{15/5} = 8{,}000 \cdot 2^{3} = 64{,}000$.
Question 2 EASY
A radioactive substance decays by halving every $6$ years. If initially there were $100$ grams, how many grams remain after $18$ years?
  • A) 6.25
  • B) 12.50
  • C) 25.00
  • D) 50.00
Explanation:
Step 1: Amount after time t is $N(t) = N_0\left(\tfrac12\right)^{t/6}$. Here $N_0 = 100$, $t = 18$, so exponent $t/6 = 3$. Step 2: $N(18) = 100\left(\tfrac12\right)^3 = 100 \cdot \tfrac{1}{8} = 12.5$ grams. Answer: B.
Question 3 EASY
An investment earns a fixed annual rate $r$ and the amount with compound interest is $A(t) = P(1+r)^t$, while with simple interest it is $S(t) = P(1+rt)$. Which statement is true for $t > 0$?
  • A) $A(t)$ grows exponentially while $S(t)$ grows linearly.
  • B) $A(t)$ grows linearly while $S(t)$ grows exponentially.
  • C) Both $A(t)$ and $S(t)$ grow exponentially.
  • D) Both stay constant as $t$ increases.
Explanation:
Step 1: $A(t) = P(1+r)^t$ is an exponential function of $t$, since the variable $t$ is in an exponent. Step 2: $S(t) = P(1+rt)$ is linear in $t$. Therefore, $A(t)$ grows exponentially while $S(t)$ grows linearly. Answer: A.
Question 4 EASY
Evaluate $\log_2 8 + \log_2 4$.
  • A) 3
  • B) 5
  • C) 4
  • D) 6
Explanation:
Step 1: Use the product rule: $\log_b x + \log_b y = \log_b(xy)$ (base $b=2$). Step 2: $\log_2 8 + \log_2 4 = \log_2(8\cdot 4) = \log_2 32 = 5$. Answer: B.
Question 5 EASY
Simplify the rational expression $\dfrac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 - 3x}$, with the restriction that $x eq 0$ and $x eq 3$.

  • B) $\dfrac{x-3}{x}$
  • C) $\dfrac{x+3}{x-3}$
  • D) $\dfrac{x-3}{x+3}$
Explanation:
Step 1: Factor numerator and denominator: $x^2 - 9 = (x-3)(x+3)$ and $x^2 - 3x = x(x-3)$. Step 2: Cancel the common factor $(x-3)$ (valid for $x eq 3$), yielding $\dfrac{x+3}{x}$. Answer: A.
Question 6 EASY
Compute $i^6 + i^3$, where $i^2 = -1$ and $i$ is the imaginary unit.
  • A) -1 - i
  • B) 0
  • C) i
  • D) 1
Explanation:
Step 1: Use $i^4 = 1$ to reduce powers: $i^6 = i^4 \cdot i^2 = 1 \cdot (-1) = -1$. Step 2: $i^3 = i^2 \cdot i = (-1) i = -i$. Step 3: Sum: $(-1) + (-i) = -1 - i$. Answer: A.

Question 8 MEDIUM

  • A) $25$
  • B) $12.5$
  • C) $50$
  • D) $100$

Question 13 MEDIUM
Compute $i^{10}$.
  • A) $-1$
  • B) $i$
  • C) $1$
  • D) $-i$
Explanation:
Powers of i cycle every 4: $i^0=1$, $i^1=i$, $i^2=-1$, $i^3=-i$, then repeat. $10 \bmod 4 = 2$, so $i^{10}=i^2=-1$.
