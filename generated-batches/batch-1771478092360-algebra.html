  • D) x ≥ 1
Explanation:

If $x ≥ 1$, then |x-1| = x - 1 and the inequality becomes $3x - 2 ≤ 2(x - 1) + 1 = 2x - 1$, which simplifies to $x ≤ 1$. Therefore, in all cases, $x ≤ 1$ is the solution set.
