Question 20 HARD
Divide the polynomial 6x^3 + x^2 - 10x + 3 by 3x - 1. What is the quotient?
  • A) 2x^2 + x - 3
  • B) 2x^2 - x + 3
  • C) 2x^2 + 2x - 1
  • D) x^2 + 1
Explanation:
Perform polynomial long division: (6x^3 + x^2 - 10x + 3) ÷ (3x - 1). First term: 6x^3 ÷ 3x = 2x^2. Multiply: (3x - 1)(2x^2) = 6x^3 - 2x^2. Subtract: (6x^3 + x^2) - (6x^3 - 2x^2) = 3x^2. Bring down -10x. Next term: 3x^2 ÷ 3x = x. Multiply: (3x - 1)(x) = 3x^2 - x. Subtract: (3x^2 - 10x) - (3x^2 - x) = -9x. Bring down +3. Next term: (-9x) ÷ (3x) = -3. Multiply: (3x - 1)(-3) = -9x + 3. Subtract: (-9x + 3) - (-9x + 3) = 0. So the quotient is 2x^2 + x - 3 and remainder 0.
