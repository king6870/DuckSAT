Question 1 EASY
In a class, five quiz scores are $70$, $85$, $90$, $75$, and $80$. What is the mean score?
  • A) $75$
  • B) $80$
  • C) $85$
  • D) $90$
Explanation:
Sum the scores: 70 + 85 + 90 + 75 + 80 = 400. There are 5 scores, so the mean is $\dfrac{400}{5} = 80$. Therefore, the correct answer is $80$.

  • C) $100\\%$
  • D) $125\\%$

Question 4 MEDIUM
In a math class of 6 students, the quiz scores are 68, 72, 72, 75, 80, and 92. What is the mean score of the class? Use the definition $\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum x_i}{n}$.
  • A) 76.5
  • B) 75
  • C) 77
  • D) 78
Explanation:
Step 1: Sum the scores: $68 + 72 + 72 + 75 + 80 + 92 = 459$. Step 2: There are $n = 6$ scores. Step 3: Using the definition $\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum x_i}{n}$, we have $\bar{x} = \dfrac{459}{6} = 76.5$. Therefore, the mean score is 76.5.
