Question 66 HARD
A population starts at 100 individuals. Model E: grows by 10% per year, compounded annually (factor $1$.10$ per year). Model L: increases by 100 individuals per year (linear). After 5 years, which model yields a larger population?
  • A) Model E
  • B) Model L
  • C) They are equal
  • D) Not enough information
Explanation:
Model E after 5 years: $100 \cdot (1.10)^5 \approx 161.05$. Model L after 5 years: $100 + 5\cdot 100 = 600$. Since $600 > 161.05$, Model L yields a larger population after 5 years. Therefore, the correct choice is B.

Question 69 HARD
If $\\log_2(x) + \\log_2(x-3) = 3$, what is the value of $x$? (Domain: $x>3$)
  • A) 4
  • B) -2
  • C) 5
  • D) 3
Explanation:
Combine logs: $\\log_2(x(x-3)) = 3 \Rightarrow x(x-3) = 2^3 = 8$. Solve $x^2 - 3x - 8 = 0$ → $(x-4)(x+2)=0$, so $x=4$ or $x=-2$. Domain requires $x>3$, so only $x=4$ is valid. Correct is A.

  • C) $\dfrac{4x+2}{x^2-1}$

eq \pm 1$.

Question 72 HARD

  • A) 1
  • B) i
  • C) -1
  • D) -i
