Question 1 EASY
In triangle $\triangle ABC$, $\angle A = $ $30^\circ$, $\angle B = $ $60^\circ$. What is $\angle C$?
  • A) $60^\circ$
  • B) $90^\circ$
  • C) $150^\circ$
  • D) $120^\circ$
Explanation:
Step 1: In any triangle, the sum of interior angles is $180^\circ$. Step 2: $\angle C = 180^\circ - (\angle A + \angle B) = 180^\circ - (30^\circ + 60^\circ) = 90^\circ$. Therefore, $\angle C = 90^\circ$.
Question 2 EASY
In a circle with center $O$, radius $r = 4$, the arc AB subtends a central angle of $60^\circ$. What is the length of arc AB?
  • A) $8\pi$
  • B) $4\pi/3$
  • C) $2\pi/3$
  • D) $4\pi$
Explanation:
Arc length formula: $L = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \cdot 2\pi r$. With $\theta = 60^\circ$ and $r = 4$, $L = \frac{60}{360} \cdot 2\pi \cdot 4 = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 8\pi = \frac{4\pi}{3}$.
Question 3 EASY
In the coordinate plane, the distance between $A(1,2)$ and $B(4,6)$ is:
  • A) 3
  • B) 5
  • C) 7
  • D) 9
Explanation:
Distance formula: $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$. Here $dx = 4-1 = 3$, $dy = 6-2 = 4$. So $d = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9+16} = \sqrt{25} = 5$.
Question 4 EASY
In a trapezoid with bases of lengths 6 and 4 and height 3, what is the area?
  • A) 9
  • B) 15
  • C) 18
  • D) 30
Explanation:
Area of a trapezoid: $A = \frac{b_1 + b_2}{2} \cdot h$. Here $b_1=6$, $b_2=4$, $h=3$: $A = \frac{6+4}{2} \cdot 3 = 5 \cdot 3 = 15$.
Question 5 EASY
In a straight line, angles $\angle A$ and $\angle B$ are supplementary. If $\angle A = 110^\circ$, what is $\angle B$?
  • A) $70^\circ$
  • B) $110^\circ$
  • C) $180^\circ$
  • D) $90^\circ$
Explanation:
Supplementary angles on a straight line sum to $180^\circ$. So $\angle B = 180^\circ - \angle A = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ$.

Question 7 HARD
In triangle $\triangle ABC$, $\angle C = 90°$, $AB = 10$, and $\angle A = 30°$. Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $C$ to $AB$. What is $AD$?
  • A) 7.5
  • B) 2.5
  • C) 5
  • D) 3.75
Explanation:
In a right triangle with hypotenuse AB = 10 and $\angle A = 30°$, the side opposite $A$ is $BC = AB\cdot \sin 30° = 10 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 5$, and the adjacent side is $AC = AB\cos 30° = 10 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 5\sqrt{3}$. The altitude from the right angle to the hypotenuse divides AB into segments with $AD = \dfrac{AC^{2}}{AB}$ and $DB = \dfrac{BC^{2}}{AB}$ (a property of right triangles: the altitude to the hypotenuse yields these segment lengths). Thus $AD = \dfrac{(5\sqrt{3})^{2}}{10} = \dfrac{75}{10} = 7.5$. So the correct answer is 7.5.
Question 8 HARD
In circle with center $O$ and radius $6$, the central angle $\angle AOB = 120°$. What is the area of sector $AOB$?
  • A) 12π
  • B) 36π
  • C) 9√3
  • D) 18π
Explanation:
Area of a sector with central angle $\theta$ (in radians) is $\tfrac{1}{2} r^{2} \theta$. Here $r=6$ and $\theta = 120° = \tfrac{2\pi}{3}$, so area = $\tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6^{2} \cdot \tfrac{2\pi}{3} = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 36 \cdot \tfrac{2\pi}{3} = 12π$.
Question 9 HARD
Given $A(1,2)$ and $B(5,-4)$, what is the equation of the perpendicular bisector of segment $\overline{AB}$?
  • A) $y = \tfrac{2}{3}x - 3$
  • B) $y = -\tfrac{2}{3}x + 3$
  • C) $y = \tfrac{3}{2}x - 1$
  • D) $y = -\tfrac{3}{2}x + 1$
Explanation:
Midpoint $M$ of $AB$ is $\left(\dfrac{1+5}{2}, \dfrac{2+(-4)}{2}\right) = (3,-1)$. Slope of $AB$ is $m_{AB} = \dfrac{-4-2}{5-1} = -\dfrac{6}{4} = -\dfrac{3}{2}$. The slope of the perpendicular bisector is $m_{perp} = -\dfrac{1}{m_{AB}} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. Using point-slope form with point $(3,-1)$: $y - (-1) = \dfrac{2}{3}(x - 3)$, which simplifies to $y = \dfrac{2}{3}x - 3$. Therefore, option A is correct.
Question 10 HARD
Triangle with coordinates $A(0,0)$, $B(8,0)$, $C(0,6)$ undergoes two transformations: first a rotation of $90°$ clockwise about the origin, then a translation by the vector $(2,3)$. What are the coordinates of the image of point $B$ after these transformations?
  • A) (2, -5)
  • B) (-2, 5)
  • C) (-5, -2)
  • D) (5, -2)
Explanation:
Rotate $B(8,0)$ clockwise by $90°$: $(x,y) \to (y,-x)$ gives $(8,0) \to (0,-8)$. Then translate by $(2,3)$: $(0,-8) \to (2,-5)$. So the image of $B$ is $(2,-5)$, which is option A.
