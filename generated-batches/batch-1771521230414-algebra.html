Question 19 HARD
A theater sold x adult tickets and y student tickets. The total number of tickets sold is 50, and adult tickets cost $12 while student tickets cost $7. If the total revenue was $540, how many adult and student tickets were sold? Solve the system: x + y = 50, 12x + 7y = 540.$
  • A) 38 adults and 12 students
  • B) 40 adults and 10 students
  • C) 30 adults and 20 students
  • D) 25 adults and 25 students
Explanation:
Let $x$ be adults and $y$ be students. From $x + y = 50$, we have $y = 50 - x$. Substitute into $12x + 7y = 540$: $12x + 7(50 - x) = 540$ → $12x + 350 - 7x = 540$ → $5x = 190$ → $x = 38$. Then $y = 50 - 38 = 12$. So there were 38 adults and 12 students.
