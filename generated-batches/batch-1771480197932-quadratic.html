Question 1 MEDIUM
Factor and solve the quadratic: $2x^2 - 5x - 3 = 0$. What are the roots?
  • A) x = 3 and x = -$\frac{1}{2}$
  • B) x = -3 and x = $\frac{1}{2}$
  • C) x = $\frac{3}{2}$ or x = -1
  • D) x = -$\frac{3}{2}$ or x = 1
Explanation:
Factor the polynomial: $2x^2 - 5x - 3 = (2x+1)(x-3) = 0$. Using zero-product property, set each factor to zero: 2x+1=0 or x-3=0, giving $x = -\frac{1}{2}$ or $x = 3$. Therefore the roots are $x=3$ and $x=-\frac{1}{2}$.
Question 2 MEDIUM
Complete the square: Solve $x^2 + 4x - 5 = 0$ by completing the square.
  • A) x = 1 or x = -5
  • B) x = -1 or x = 5
  • C) x = 0 or x = -4
  • D) x = 2 or x = -6
Explanation:
Rewrite: $x^2 + 4x - 5 = 0$ → $x^2 + 4x + 4 - 4 - 5 = 0$ → $(x+2)^2 = 9$. Then $x+2 = \pm 3$, so $x = 1$ or $x = -5$.
Question 3 MEDIUM
Solve by the quadratic formula: For $3x^2 - 2x - 8 = 0$, what are the roots?
  • A) x = 2 or x = -$\frac{4}{3}$
  • B) x = -$\frac{2}{3}$ or x = $\frac{4}{3}$
  • C) x = $\frac{4}{3}$ or x = -2
  • D) x = -2 or x = $\frac{4}{3}$
Explanation:
With $a=3$, $b=-2$, $c=-8$, discriminant $\Delta = (-2)^2 - 4(3)(-8) = 4 + 96 = 100$. Then $x =\frac{2 \pm \sqrt{100}}{2\cdot 3} = \frac{2 \pm 10}{6}$, giving $x = \frac{12}{6}=2$ or $x = \frac{-8}{6} = -\frac{4}{3}$.
Question 4 MEDIUM

  • A) Vertex (4,5); axis of symmetry x = 4; minimum value 5

  • C) Vertex (4,-5); axis of symmetry x = 4; minimum -5
  • D) Vertex (-4,5); axis of symmetry x = -4; minimum 5
Explanation:
This is in vertex form $y = a(x-h)^2 + k$ with $a=2>0$, $h=4$, $k=5$. The vertex is $(h,k)=(4,5)$ and the axis of symmetry is $x=h=4$. Since $a>0$, the parabola opens upward and has a minimum value $k=5$.
Question 5 MEDIUM
Rewrite $-x^2 + 6x - 5 = 0$ in vertex form by completing the square; what is the vertex of the corresponding parabola?
  • A) Vertex (3, 4); axis x = 3; maximum value 4
  • B) Vertex (-3, 4); axis x = -3; maximum value 4
  • C) Vertex (3, -4); axis x = 3; minimum -4
  • D) Vertex (-3, -4); axis x = -3; minimum -4
Explanation:
Complete the square: $-x^2 + 6x - 5 = -(x^2 - 6x) - 5 = -[x^2 - 6x + 9] + 9 - 5 = -(x-3)^2 + 4$. Thus the parabola has vertex $(3,4)$, axis of symmetry $x=3$, and maximum value $4$ (since the parabola opens downward).
Question 6 MEDIUM
Projectile motion: The height of a launched object is modeled by $y = -5t^2 + 40t$, where $t$ is time in seconds. What is the maximum height and when is it reached?
  • A) Maximum height 80 meters at t = 4 seconds
  • B) Maximum height 60 meters at t = 6 seconds
  • C) Maximum height 100 meters at t = 5 seconds
  • D) Maximum height 40 meters at t = 2 seconds
Explanation:
For $y = -5t^2 + 40t$, a = -5 and b = 40. The vertex occurs at $t = -\frac{b}{2a} = -\frac{40}{2(-5)} = 4$. The maximum height is $y(4) = -5(16) + 40(4) = -80 + 160 = 80$ meters. So the maximum height is 80 m at t = 4 s.

Question 9 MEDIUM
Solve by completing the square: $x^2 - 6x + 5 = 0$.
  • A) x = 1 or x = 5
  • B) x = -1 or x = -5
  • C) x = 2 or x = 3
  • D) x = 0 or x = -5
Explanation:
Complete the square: $x^2 - 6x + 5 = 0 \Rightarrow x^2 - 6x + 9 - 9 + 5 = 0 \Rightarrow (x-3)^2 - 4 = 0 \Rightarrow (x-3)^2 = 4 \Rightarrow x-3 = \pm 2 \Rightarrow x = 1$ or $x = 5$.
Question 10 MEDIUM
Area optimization: A rectangle has sides $x$ and $20 - x$ (perimeter fixed at 40). Express area as a quadratic and determine the maximum area and the side length that achieves it.
  • A) Maximum area 100 square units at x = 10
  • B) Maximum area 80 at x = 8
  • C) Maximum area 120 at x = 9
  • D) Maximum area 60 at x = 5
Explanation:
Area $A(x) = x(20 - x) = -x^2 + 20x$, a downward parabola. The vertex occurs at $x = -\frac{b}{2a} = -\frac{20}{2(-1)} = 10$. Then $A_{max} = A(10) = 100$ square units.

Question 13 HARD
For the quadratic equation $2x^2 + 3x - 2 = 0$, use the quadratic formula to determine the real solutions and the number of real roots.
  • A) Two real solutions: $x = \frac{1}{2}$ and $x = -2$
  • B) One real solution: $x = -\frac{3}{4}$ (double root)
  • C) No real solutions
  • D) Two real solutions: $x = 2$ and $x = -1$
Explanation:
Here $a=2$, $b=3$, $c=-2$. The discriminant is $\Delta=b^2-4ac=9-4(2)(-2)=25>0$, so two real roots. $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{\Delta}}{2a} = \frac{-3 \pm 5}{4}$, yielding $x = \frac{1}{2}$ and $x = -2$.
Question 14 HARD
A stone is thrown upward from a 5-foot platform with initial velocity 48 ft/s. The height (in feet) after t seconds is h(t) = -16 t^2 + 48 t + 5. Approximately how long will it take to hit the ground (h(t) = 0)?
  • A) Approximately 3.10 seconds
  • B) Approximately 0.10 seconds
  • C) Approximately 1.50 seconds
  • D) Never hits the ground
Explanation:
Set h(t)=0: $-16t^2+48t+5=0$. Multiply by -1: $16t^2-48t-5=0$. Discriminant: $\Delta=(-48)^2-4\cdot 16 \cdot (-5)=2304+320=2624=64\cdot 41$, so $\sqrt{\Delta}=8\sqrt{41}$. Solutions: $t=\frac{48\pm 8\sqrt{41}}{32}=\frac{6\pm \sqrt{41}}{4}$. The positive root is $t=\frac{6+\sqrt{41}}{4}\approx 3.10$ s (the negative root is nonphysical).
