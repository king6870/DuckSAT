Solve by factoring: if $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$, what are the real solutions for x?
  • A) x = 2 or x = 3
  • B) x = -2 or x = -3
  • C) x = 1 or x = 6
  • D) x = 0 or x = 5
Explanation:
Factor $x^2 - 5x + 6$ as $(x-2)(x-3)=0$. By the zero-product property, the solutions satisfy x-2=0 or x-3=0. Therefore x = 2 or x = 3.

Question 3 MEDIUM
Solve $2x^2 + 3x - 2 = 0$ using the quadratic formula. What are the solutions?
  • A) $x = \frac{1}{2}$ or $x = -2$
  • B) $x = -\frac{1}{2}$ or $x = 2$
  • C) $x = 1$ or $x = -4$
  • D) $x = -1$ or $x = 4$
Explanation:
Here $a=2$, $b=3$, $c=-2$. The discriminant $D=b^2-4ac=9-4(2)(-2)=9+16=25$. So $x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{D}}{2a}=\frac{-3\pm5}{4}$ giving $x=\frac{1}{2}$ or $x=-2$.

Question 13 HARD
A projectile's height is modeled by $h(t) = -4t^2 + 60t$, where $t$ is time in seconds and height in meters. How many real solutions does $h(t) = 0$ have, and at what times does the projectile hit the ground?
  • A) There are $2$ real solutions: $t = 0$ and $t = 15$
  • B) There is $1$ real solution: $t = 0$
  • C) There are $0$ real solutions
  • D) There are $3$ real solutions
Explanation:
Set $h(t)=0$: $-4t^2 + 60t = 0 \Rightarrow -4t(t - 15) = 0$. The real solutions are $t = 0$ and $t = 15$, so there are two real solutions. The discriminant analysis with $a = -4$, $b = 60$, $c = 0$ yields $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac = 3600 > 0$, confirming two real roots: $t = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{\Delta}}{2a} = \frac{-60 \pm 60}{-8}$ which gives $t = 0$ and $t = 15$.
