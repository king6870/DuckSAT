Question 1 EASY
Factor and solve the quadratic: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$.
  • A) $x = 2$ or $x = 3$
  • B) $x = -2$ or $x = -3$
  • C) $x = 1$ or $x = 6$
  • D) $x = 0$ or $x = 5$
Explanation:
Step 1: Factor the quadratic: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3)$. Step 2: By the zero-product property, $x-2=0$ or $x-3=0$. Step 3: Solve: $x=2$ or $x=3$.

Explanation:

Step 3: Vertex form is $R(x) = -(x-3)^2 + 14$, so the vertex is $(h,k)=(3,14)$. The maximum revenue is $14$ (thousand dollars) attained when $x=3$ (thousand units).
Question 4 MEDIUM
Factor the quadratic $2x^2 - 5x - 3 = 0$ and determine all real solutions.
  • A) $x = 3$ or $x = -\frac{1}{2}$
  • B) $x = -3$ or $x = \frac{1}{2}$
  • C) $x = -\frac{3}{2}$ or $x = 1$
  • D) $x = 0$ or $x = \frac{3}{2}$
Explanation:
Step-by-step: Factor the polynomial: $2x^2 - 5x - 3 = (2x+1)(x-3)$. By the zero-product property, set each factor to zero: $2x+1=0$ yields $x=-\frac{1}{2}$; $x-3=0$ yields $x=3$. Therefore the real solutions are $x=3$ and $x=-\frac{1}{2}$.
Question 5 MEDIUM
Rewrite the quadratic $2x^2 + 8x + 3$ in vertex form by completing the square.
  • A) $2(x+2)^2 - 5$
  • B) $2(x-2)^2 + 5$
  • C) $2(x+2)^2 + 3$
  • D) -2(x+2)^2 + 5
Explanation:
Complete the square: $2x^2 + 8x + 3 = 2(x^2 + 4x) + 3 = 2[(x+2)^2 - 4] + 3 = 2(x+2)^2 - 8 + 3 = 2(x+2)^2 - 5$. So the vertex form is $2(x+2)^2 - 5$ with vertex $(-2,-5)$.
Question 6 MEDIUM
For the equation $3x^2 - 7x + 2 = 0$, how many real solutions are there? Use the discriminant.
  • A) 0 real solutions
  • B) 1 real solution
  • C) 2 real solutions
  • D) Infinitely many
Explanation:
Compute the discriminant: $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac = (-7)^2 - 4(3)(2) = 49 - 24 = 25$. Since $\Delta > 0$, there are two real solutions. The roots are $x = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{25}}{6} = \frac{7 \pm 5}{6}$, giving $x = 2$ or $x = \frac{1}{3}$.
