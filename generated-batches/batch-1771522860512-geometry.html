Question 1 EASY
In the right triangle $\triangle ABC$ with $\angle B = 90°$, $AB = 3$, and $BC = 4$. What is $AC$?
  • A) 5
  • B) 6
  • C) $\sqrt{13}$
  • D) 7
Explanation:
Because $\angle B = 90°$, by the Pythagorean theorem in $\triangle ABC$ we have $AB^2 + BC^2 = AC^2$. Substituting gives $3^2 + 4^2 = AC^2 \Rightarrow 9 + 16 = AC^2 \Rightarrow AC^2 = 25 \Rightarrow AC = \sqrt{25} = 5$.

In right triangle $\triangle ABC$ with $\angle C = 90°$, $\angle A = 30°$, and $AB = 12$, let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $C$ to $AB$. What is the length of $CD$?
  • A) $3\sqrt{3}$
  • B) $6\sqrt{3}$
  • C) $6$
  • D) $12$
Explanation:
Step 1: Recognize a 30°-60°-90° triangle. With $\angle C = 90°$ and $\angle A = 30°$, the hypotenuse is $AB = 12$, so the short leg opposite $30°$ is $a = AB/2 = 6$ and the long leg is $b = AB\sqrt{3}/2 = 6\sqrt{3}$. Step 2: The altitude to the hypotenuse in a right triangle satisfies $CD = \dfrac{ab}{c}$ where $a$ and $b$ are the legs and $c$ is the hypotenuse. Thus $CD = \dfrac{(6)(6\sqrt{3})}{12} = \dfrac{36\sqrt{3}}{12} = 3\sqrt{3}$. Therefore, the correct answer is $3\sqrt{3}$.

In circle $O$ with radius $5$, radii $OA$ and $OB$ form a central angle $\angle AOB = 60°$. Find the area of the circular segment $AB$ (the region between chord $AB$ and arc $AB$).
  • A) $$25$\pi/6$ - 25$\sqrt{3}/4$$$
  • B) $25\pi/6$
  • C) $25\sqrt{3}/4$
  • D) $0$
Explanation:
Step 1: Sector area for central angle $60°$ with radius $5$ is $\dfrac{60°}{360°} \cdot \pi r^2 = \dfrac{1}{6} \pi (5^2) = \dfrac{25\pi}{6}$. Step 2: Area of triangle $AOB$ is $\dfrac{1}{2} OA\cdot OB \sin\angle AOB = \dfrac{1}{2} (5)(5) \sin 60° = \dfrac{25}{2} \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \dfrac{25\sqrt{3}}{4}$. Step 3: Circular segment area = sector area − triangle area = $\dfrac{25\pi}{6} - \dfrac{25\sqrt{3}}{4}$.

Step 3: Distance from $C(-2,6)$ to the line $3x+2y-11=0$ is $\dfrac{|3(-2) + 2(6) - 11|}{\sqrt{3^2+2^2}} = \dfrac{|-6 + 12 - 11|}{\sqrt{13}} = \dfrac{5}{\sqrt{13}}$.
