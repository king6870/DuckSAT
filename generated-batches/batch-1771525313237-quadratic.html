Question 1 HARD
Factor and solve the quadratic equation $2x^2 - 7x + 3 = 0$ using the zero-product property.
  • A) $x = \frac{1}{2}$ and $x = 3$
  • B) $x = -\frac{1}{2}$ and $x = 3$
  • C) $x = \frac{1}{2}$ or $x = -3$
  • D) $x = -\frac{1}{2}$ and $x = -3$
Explanation:
Factor $2x^2 - 7x + 3$ as $(2x - 1)(x - 3)$. Expanding gives $2x^2 - 6x - x + 3 = 2x^2 - 7x + 3$. Thus $(2x - 1)(x - 3) = 0$, so by the zero-product property $2x - 1 = 0$ or $x - 3 = 0$, yielding $x = \tfrac{1}{2}$ and $x = 3$.
Question 2 HARD
For $f(x) = 3x^2 - 12x + 4$, rewrite in vertex form by completing the square and identify the vertex.
  • A) $f(x) = 3(x-2)^2 - 8$
  • B) $f(x) = 3(x+2)^2 - 8$
  • C) $f(x) = 3(x-2)^2 + 4$
  • D) $f(x) = 3(x-2)^2 - 4$
Explanation:
Factor the leading coefficient: $3x^2 - 12x + 4 = 3(x^2 - 4x) + 4$. Complete the square: $x^2 - 4x = (x-2)^2 - 4$. So $f(x) = 3[(x-2)^2 - 4] + 4 = 3(x-2)^2 - 12 + 4 = 3(x-2)^2 - 8$. The vertex form is $f(x) = 3(x-2)^2 - 8$ with vertex at $(2, -8)$.
Question 3 HARD
For the equation $4x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0$, which statement correctly describes its discriminant and number of real solutions?
  • A) $\Delta = 0$; exactly one real solution
  • B) $\Delta > 0$; two real solutions
  • C) $\Delta < 0$; no real solutions
  • D) $\Delta = 1$; two real solutions
Explanation:
Here $a = 4$, $b = -4$, $c = 1$. The discriminant is $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac = (-4)^2 - 4(4)(1) = 16 - 16 = 0$. A zero discriminant means exactly one real solution (a repeated root).
Question 4 HARD
A company's profit P(x) in dollars from selling x units is modeled by the vertex form $P(x) = -2\,(x-5)^2 + 60$. What is the maximum profit and at how many units should be sold to achieve it?
  • A) Maximum profit 60 dollars at 5 units
  • B) Maximum profit 60 dollars at 0 units
  • C) Maximum profit 120 dollars at 5 units
  • D) Maximum profit 60 dollars at -5 units
Explanation:
In vertex form $P(x) = a(x-h)^2 + k$, the vertex is $(h,k)$. Here $h = 5$, $k = 60$, and $a = -2 < 0$, so the parabola opens downward making the vertex a maximum. Thus the maximum profit is $P(5) = 60$ dollars, achieved at 5 units sold.
