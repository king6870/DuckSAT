Question 1 EASY
In a survey of $200$ students, $120$ prefer coffee, $60$ prefer tea, and $20$ prefer water. What is the ratio of the number of students who prefer coffee to the number who prefer tea?
  • A) $2:1$
  • B) $1:2$
  • C) $2:3$
  • D) $3:1$
Explanation:
Count coffee = 120, tea = 60. The ratio coffee:tea is $120:60$, which simplifies to $2:1$ since $120 \div 60 = 2$. Therefore the correct ratio is $2:1$.
Question 2 EASY
In a poll of $80$ voters, $30$ support candidate A, $25$ support candidate B, and $25$ are undecided. What is the probability that a randomly selected voter supports A or B?
  • A) $11/16$
  • B) $0.70$
  • C) $0.625$
  • D) $0.50$
Explanation:
Let $P(A \cup B) = \frac{30+25}{80} = \frac{55}{80} = \frac{11}{16} = 0.6875$.
Question 3 MEDIUM
A dataset of eight quiz scores: 62, 75, 80, 85, 90, 92, 95, 100. If another student scores 70, what is the mean of the nine scores?
  • A) 83.22
  • B) 84.88
  • C) 85.50
  • D) 83.00
Explanation:
Step 1: Sum of original eight scores is $62 + 75 + 80 + 85 + 90 + 92 + 95 + 100 = 679$. Step 2: Original mean is $\frac{679}{8} = 84.875$. Step 3: After adding 70, new total is $679 + 70 = 749$ and new count is $9$, so the new mean is $\frac{749}{9} \approx 83.222\\!\overline{2}$. Therefore the nine-score mean is approximately $83.22$.
Question 4 MEDIUM
In a class of 200 students, 120 prefer coffee. Suppose two students are selected at random with replacement. What is the probability that both prefer coffee?
  • A) 0.36 (36%)
  • B) 0.18 (18%)
  • C) 0.12 (12%)
  • D) 0.64 (64%)
Explanation:
Step 1: Probability a randomly chosen student prefers coffee is $\frac{120}{200} = 0.60$. Step 2: Since selection is with replacement, the two draws are independent, so $P("both\\ coffee") = 0.60 \times 0.60 = 0.36$. Step 3: Expressed as a percent, that's 36%.
Question 5 MEDIUM
A scatter plot of study hours (x) versus exam score (y) has a line of best fit approximated by $y = 3x + 40$. A student studied 7 hours and scored 70. What is the residual (actual minus predicted) for this student?
  • A) -9
  • B) 9
  • C) 49
  • D) 61
Explanation:
Step 1: Predicted score for x = 7 is $\hat{y} = 3(7) + 40 = 21 + 40 = 61$. Step 2: Residual = actual - predicted = $70 - 61 = 9$. Answer is 9, a positive residual indicating the actual score is higher than predicted by the line of best fit.
