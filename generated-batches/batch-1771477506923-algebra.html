Question 3 MEDIUM
Solve the system: $2x + y = 7$ and $x - y = 1$.
  • A) $x = \frac{8}{3},\; y = \frac{5}{3}$
  • B) $x = 3,\; y = 1$
  • C) $x = 4,\; y = 3$
  • D) $x = \frac{5}{2},\; y = \frac{1}{2}$
Explanation:
From the second equation, $y = x - 1$. Substitute into the first: $2x + (x - 1) = 7 \Rightarrow 3x - 1 = 7 \Rightarrow 3x = 8 \Rightarrow x = \frac{8}{3}$. Then $y = x - 1 = \frac{8}{3} - \frac{3}{3} = \frac{5}{3}$.

  • B) width 5 cm, length 7 cm
  • C) width 6 cm, length 8 cm
  • D) width 7 cm, length 9 cm
Explanation:
Perimeter $P = 2(l + w) = 30$ and $l = w + 2$. So $2((w+2) + w) = 30 \Rightarrow 4w + 4 = 30 \Rightarrow 4w = 26 \Rightarrow w = 6.5$. Then $l = w + 2 = 8.5$.

Question 13 HARD
Solve the system: $2x + 3y = 12$ and $4x - y = 5$.
  • A) $(\frac{27}{14},\frac{19}{7})$
  • B) $(1, 2)$
  • C) $(0,12)$
  • D) $(\tfrac{3}{2}, \tfrac{3}{2})$
Explanation:
From the second equation, $y=4x-5$. Substitute into the first: $2x + 3(4x-5) = 12 \Rightarrow 2x + 12x - 15 = 12 \Rightarrow 14x = 27 \Rightarrow x = \tfrac{27}{14}$. Then $y = 4(\tfrac{27}{14}) - 5 = \tfrac{108}{14} - 5 = \tfrac{54}{7} - \tfrac{35}{7} = \tfrac{19}{7}$. So the solution is $(x,y) = (\tfrac{27}{14}, \tfrac{19}{7})$.
Question 14 HARD
A company sells a product with revenue modeled by $R(x) = -x^2 + 10x$, where $x$ is the number of units sold (with $0 \le x \le 10$). Which value of $x$ maximizes revenue?
  • A) 5
  • B) 0
  • C) 10
  • D) 2
Explanation:
Rewrite: $R(x) = -x^2 + 10x = -(x^2 - 10x) = -(x^2 - 10x + 25) + 25 = -(x-5)^2 + 25$. This is a downward-opening parabola with vertex at $x = 5$, giving maximum revenue $R(5) = 25$. Therefore, $x=5$ maximizes revenue.
