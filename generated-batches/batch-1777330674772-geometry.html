Question 1 EASY
In right triangle $\triangle ABC$ with right angle at $C$, given $\angle A = 30°$ and $AB = 10$, what is the length of $BC$?
  • A) $5$
  • B) $5\sqrt{3}$
  • C) $10$
  • D) $\sqrt{3}$
Explanation:
Since $\angle A = 30°$, the triangle is a $30$-$60$-$90$ right triangle. The sides are in the ratio $1:\sqrt{3}:2$ with the hypotenuse opposite $90°$. Here $AB$ is the hypotenuse and equals $10$, so the side opposite $\angle A$ (which is $BC$) has length $10 \times \frac{1}{2} = 5$.
Question 2 EASY
Find the slope of the line through the points $ (1,2) $ and $ (4,8) $.
  • A) $1$
  • B) $2$
  • C) $3$
  • D) $4$
Explanation:
The slope is $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{8 - 2}{4 - 1} = \frac{6}{3} = 2$.

In $\triangle ABC$, $\angle C = 90^{\circ}$, $\angle A = 30^{\circ}$, $\angle B = 60^{\circ}$, and $\overline{AB} = 6$. What is the area of $\triangle ABC$?
  • A) $\frac{9\sqrt{3}}{2}$
  • B) $\frac{27}{2}$
  • C) $9\sqrt{3}$
  • D) $\frac{9\sqrt{3}}{4}$
Explanation:
Step 1: In a 30-60-90 right triangle with hypotenuse $\overline{AB} = 6$, the side opposite the 30° angle ($\overline{BC}$) is half the hypotenuse, so $BC = \frac{AB}{2} = 3$. Step 2: The side opposite the 60° angle ($\overline{AC}$) is $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ times the hypotenuse, so $AC = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} AB = 3\sqrt{3}$. Step 3: The area is $\tfrac{1}{2} \cdot BC \cdot AC = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 3\sqrt{3} = \frac{9\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

So $x = \frac{12 \pm 4\sqrt{19}}{10} = \frac{6 \pm 2\sqrt{19}}{5}$. Step 2: The two intersection points have coordinates with a difference $\Delta x = x_1 - x_2 = \frac{4\sqrt{19}}{5}$ and $\Delta y = y_1 - y_2 = 2\Delta x = \frac{8\sqrt{19}}{5}$. Step 3: The chord length is $D = \sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{4\sqrt{19}}{5}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{8\sqrt{19}}{5}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\frac{80 \\, 19}{25}} = \frac{4}{5}\sqrt{95}$.
Question 5 MEDIUM
A rectangle with width 8 and height 5 has a right triangle with legs 3 and 4 removed from its top-left corner (the triangle's legs lie along the rectangle's top and left edges). What is the area of the remaining figure?
  • A) 40
  • B) 6
  • C) 34
  • D) 36
Explanation:
Step 1: Area of the full rectangle = 8 × 5 = 40. Step 2: Area of the removed right triangle = (1/2) × 3 × 4 = 6. Step 3: Remaining area = 40 − 6 = 34.
