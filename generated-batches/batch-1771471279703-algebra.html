Question 15 MEDIUM
Divide $2x^3 + 3x^2 - x + 5$ by $x + 2$. What is the quotient and remainder?
  • A) $2x^2 - x + 1$ with remainder 3
  • B) $2x^2 + x - 2$ with remainder 7
  • C) $2x^2 - 2x + 5$ with remainder -1
  • D) $x^2 + x + 3$ with remainder 3
Explanation:
Long division: Divide $2x^3$ by $x$ to get $2x^2$. Multiply: $(x+2)\cdot 2x^2 = 2x^3 + 4x^2$. Subtract: $(2x^3 + 3x^2 - x + 5) - (2x^3 + 4x^2) = -x^2 - x + 5$. Divide $-x^2$ by $x$ to get $-x$. Multiply: $(x+2)(-x) = -x^2 - 2x$. Subtract: $(-x^2 - x) - (-x^2 - 2x) = x$. Bring down 5. Divide $x$ by $x$ to get 1. Multiply: $(x+2)(1) = x + 2$. Subtract: $(x + 5) - (x + 2) = 3$. Thus quotient is $2x^2 - x + 1$ and remainder is 3.

Question 18 HARD
Solve the system: $x + y = 5$, $2x + 3y = 13$.
  • A) (2, 3)
  • B) (3, 2)
  • C) (1, 4)

From the first equation, y = 5 - x. Substitute into the second: $2x + 3(5 - x) = 13 \Rightarrow 2x + 15 - 3x = 13 \Rightarrow -x + 15 = 13 \Rightarrow -x = -2 \Rightarrow x = 2$. Then y = 5 - 2 = 3. So the solution is (2, 3).
