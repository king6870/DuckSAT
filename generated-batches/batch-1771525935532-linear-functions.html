  • C) $y = \frac{x}{3} + 20$
  • D) $y = 3x - 20$
Explanation:

Step 3: Check the other options: (B) wrongly uses 20 as the slope and 3 as the intercept; (C) uses division; (D) uses a negative intercept. Therefore, A is correct.

Question 3 EASY
Which statement about two lines with slopes $m_1 = 3$ and $m_2 = -\frac{1}{3}$ is true?
  • A) They are parallel.
  • B) They are perpendicular.
  • C) They intersect at the origin only.
  • D) They have the same y-intercept.
Explanation:
Step 1: Two lines are perpendicular if their slopes are negative reciprocals, i.e., $m_1 m_2 = -1$. Here $3 \cdot (-\tfrac{1}{3}) = -1$, so the lines are perpendicular. Step 2: They are not parallel since $m_1 eq m_2$. Step 3: The intersection is not necessarily at the origin. Step 4: The y-intercepts are not determined to be the same. Therefore, B is correct.
