  • A) $y = -x + 3$
  • B) $y = x + 3$
  • C) $y = x - 3$
  • D) $y = -x - 3$
Explanation:
First compute the slope of AB: $m_{AB} = ( -2 - 2 ) / ( 5 - 1 ) = -4/4 = -1$. A line perpendicular to AB has slope $m_{perp} = 1$. The midpoint is $M = ((1+5)/2, (2+(-2))/2) = (3,0)$. The line with slope 1 through (3,0) is $y - 0 = 1(x - 3)$, i.e. $y = x - 3$.

Question 20 HARD
In a regular hexagon, the circumradius equals the side length. If the circumradius is 5, what is the area of the hexagon?
  • A) $\frac{75\sqrt{3}}{2}$
  • B) $75\sqrt{3}$
  • C) $9\sqrt{3}$
  • D) $\frac{75\sqrt{3}}{4}$
Explanation:
A regular hexagon can be partitioned into six equilateral triangles of side length $s$ (which equals the hexagon’s side length and also equals the circumradius here, $s=5$). The area of one such triangle is $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}s^2$, so the hexagon’s area is $6\cdot\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} s^2 = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} s^2$. Substituting $s=5$ gives $A = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot 25 = \frac{75\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
