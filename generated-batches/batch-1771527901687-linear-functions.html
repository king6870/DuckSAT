Question 1 EASY
A taxi company charges a base fare of 4 dollars plus 2 dollars per mile. The total cost $C$ (in dollars) as a function of miles $d$ traveled is given by which equation?
  • A) $C = 2d + 4$
  • B) $C = \frac{d}{2} + 4$
  • C) $C = 4d + 2$
  • D) $C = -2d + 4$
Explanation:
In slope-intercept form $y = mx + b$, $m$ is the rate per mile and $b$ is the base fare. The problem states a base fare of 4 dollars ($b = 4$) and a per-mile rate of 2 dollars ($m = 2$). Therefore $C = 2d + 4$. Option A matches this form. Options B, C, and D use incorrect values for the slope or sign.
Question 2 EASY
Using the point-slope form $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$, a line passes through $(3, -1)$ with slope $m = 4$. Which equation is the correct point-slope form?
  • A) $y - (-1) = 4(x - 3)$
  • B) $y - (-2) = 4(x - 3)$
  • C) $y - 1 = 4(x - 3)$
  • D) $y - (-1) = 4(x - 2)$
Explanation:
Since the line passes through $(x_1, y_1) = (3, -1)$ with slope $m = 4$, substitute into $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ to obtain $y - (-1) = 4(x - 3)$. That is option A. The other options use the wrong $y_1$ or $x_1$ values.
Question 3 EASY
If a line has slope 5, which of the following lines is perpendicular to it?
  • A) $y = 5x + 7$
  • B) $y = -\frac{1}{5}x + 2$
  • C) $y = 5x - 3$
  • D) $y = \frac{1}{5}x - 1$
Explanation:
Two lines are perpendicular if their slopes multiply to -1. With the given slope $m_1 = 5$, the required slope is $m_2 = -\frac{1}{5}$, which in LaTeX is $-\frac{1}{5}$. Therefore option B is perpendicular. The other lines have slopes 5 or +1/5, not the negative reciprocal.
Question 4 EASY
A line crosses the y-axis at -8 and the x-axis at 6. What is the slope of the line?
  • A) $\frac{4}{3}$
  • B) $-\frac{4}{3}$
  • C) $\frac{6}{8}$
  • D) $-\frac{6}{8}$
Explanation:
Using the intercepts, the line passes through (0, -8) and (6, 0). The slope is $m = \frac{0 - (-8)}{6 - 0} = \frac{8}{6} = \frac{4}{3}$. The line is increasing, so the slope is positive. Options B, C, D are incorrect.
Question 5 EASY
A car-rental company charges a base fee of 22 dollars plus 0.35 dollars per mile driven. The total cost $C$ (in dollars) as a function of miles $d$ is $C = 0.35d + 22$. What is the slope of this linear relation?
  • A) $0.35$
  • B) $22$
  • C) $0.053$
  • D) $-0.35$
Explanation:
In the standard form $C = md + b$, the slope $m$ is the rate of change of cost per mile. Here $m = 0.35$ dollars per mile, and the intercept $b$ is 22 dollars. Therefore the slope is 0.35.

Let $y$ be the total cost in dollars for renting a car for one day, and let $x$ be miles driven in a day. A car rental company charges a flat daily fee of $40$ dollars and $0.15$ dollars per mile. Which equation represents this relationship? If you drive $180$ miles, what is the total cost?
  • A) $y = 0.15x + 40$
  • B) $y = 40x + 0.15$
  • C) $y = \frac{x}{0.15} + 40$
  • D) $y = 0.15x - 40$
Explanation:
The total cost is the sum of the flat daily fee and the distance cost: cost per mile times miles plus the daily fee. Here cost per mile = 0.15 and daily fee = 40, so the equation is $y = 0.15x + 40$. Therefore option A is correct. For $x = 180$ miles, $y = 0.15(180) + 40 = 27 + 40 = 67$ dollars. This confirms the calculation of the total cost.
Question 8 MEDIUM
Line L has equation $y = 2x + 5$. A line perpendicular to L has slope $-\tfrac{1}{2}$. If this perpendicular line passes through $(4,-3)$, which equation could describe it in slope-intercept form?
  • A) $y = 2x + 5$
  • B) $y = -2x - 5$
  • C) $y = -\tfrac{1}{2}x - 1$
  • D) $y = -\tfrac{1}{2}x + 4$
Explanation:
A line perpendicular to a line with slope 2 has slope $-\tfrac{1}{2}$. Using point-slope form with point (4,-3): $y - (-3) = -\tfrac{1}{2}(x - 4)$. This gives $y + 3 = -\tfrac{1}{2}x + 2$, hence $y = -\tfrac{1}{2}x - 1$. Therefore option C is correct. Option A is the original line; option B has the wrong slope; option D has the wrong intercept.
