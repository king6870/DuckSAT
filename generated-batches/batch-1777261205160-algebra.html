Question 1 EASY
Word problem: The sum of two numbers is $13$ and their difference is $5$. Let the larger be $x$ and the smaller be $y$. Which of the following pairs $ (x, y) $ satisfies the conditions?
  • A) (9, 4)
  • B) (8, 5)
  • C) (7, 6)
  • D) (10, 3)
Explanation:
From the equations $x + y = 13$ and $x - y = 5$, add them to obtain $2x = 18$, hence $x = 9$. Substitute into $x + y = 13$ to get $9 + y = 13$, so $y = 4$. Thus the pair is $(x, y) = (9, 4)$, which satisfies both equations. Therefore the correct choice is A.
Question 2 MEDIUM
Two classmates are buying notebooks and rulers. Let $x$ be the number of notebooks and $y$ the number of rulers. They buy a total of $7$ items and spend $16.50 in total. Each notebook costs $3$ and each ruler costs $1.50$. How many notebooks did they buy?$
  • A) 2 notebooks
  • B) 3 notebooks
  • C) 4 notebooks
  • D) 5 notebooks
Explanation:
Let $x$ be the number of notebooks and $y$ the number of rulers. The two equations are: $x + y = 7$ and $3x + 1.5y = 16.5$. Solve by substitution: from $x + y = 7$ we get $y = 7 - x$. Substitute: $3x + 1.5(7 - x) = 16.5$. This gives $3x + 10.5 - 1.5x = 16.5$, so $1.5x = 6$ and $x = 4$. Then $y = 3$. Therefore, they bought 4 notebooks.
