  • C) 1
  • D) 5/3
Explanation:

Question 2 EASY
Two types of tickets cost dollars: adult tickets cost A dollars and student tickets cost B dollars. In Sale 1, 6 adult tickets and 4 student tickets were sold for 42 dollars. In Sale 2, 2 adult tickets and 5 student tickets were sold for 25 dollars. What is the price of an adult ticket?
  • A) 3 dollars
  • B) 4 dollars
  • C) 5 dollars
  • D) 6 dollars
Explanation:
Let A be the adult price and B be the student price. The two sales give the system: 6A + 4B = 42 and 2A + 5B = 25. Solve by elimination: multiply the first by 2 to get 12A + 8B = 84; multiply the second by 6 to get 12A + 30B = 150. Subtract: 22B = 66 → B = 3. Substitute into 6A + 4(3) = 42 → 6A = 30 → A = 5. Therefore, the adult ticket price is 5 dollars.

  • A) $x = -8$

  • C) $x = 8$
  • D) $x = 0$
Explanation:
Start with the equation: $3(2x - 4) + 5 = 7x + 1$. Distribute: $6x - 12 + 5 = 7x + 1$, so $6x - 7 = 7x + 1$. Subtract $6x$ from both sides: $-7 = x + 1$. Subtract 1: $-8 = x$. Therefore, $x = -8$.

  • A) 15
  • B) 60
  • C) 120
  • D) 165
Explanation:
From a + s = 180, we have s = 180 - a. Substitute into 12a + 8s = 1500: 12a + 8(180 - a) = 1500. This gives 12a + 1440 - 8a = 1500 → 4a + 1440 = 1500 → 4a = 60 → a = 15. Therefore, 15 adult tickets were sold.
