Question 1 EASY
In a math class, the quiz scores for five students are: 72, 85, 91, 85, and 73. What is the mean score?
  • A) 81
  • B) 81.2
  • C) 83
  • D) 85
Explanation:
Compute the mean by summing the scores and dividing by the number of scores. Step 1: $72+85+91+85+73 = 406$. Step 2: $\frac{406}{5} = 81.2$. Therefore, the mean is 81.2, so the correct answer is B. Common mistakes: A rounds 81.2 to 81, C uses the wrong divisor or wrong sum, D uses the maximum score instead of the mean.
Question 2 EASY
A fair six-sided die is rolled once. What is the probability of rolling an even number?
  • A) 1/6
  • B) 1/2
  • C) 2/3
  • D) 1
Explanation:
There are three even outcomes: 2, 4, 6. So probability = $\frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$. Common mistakes: A) thinking of a single outcome, C) miscounting evens vs odds, D) assuming certainty.

Question 4 EASY
A store sells 150 units in January and 180 units in February. What is the percent increase from January to February?
  • A) 15%
  • B) 20%
  • C) 25%
  • D) 30%
Explanation:
Increase = 180 − 150 = 30. Percent increase = $\frac{30}{150} = 0.20 = 20\%$. Common mistakes: A) using a 15% base, C) using 120 as base, D) using 100 as base.
Question 5 EASY
If a data set has standard deviation s, what happens to the standard deviation if every value in the data set is increased by a constant 2?
  • A) It increases.
  • B) It decreases.
  • C) It stays the same.
  • D) It doubles.
Explanation:
Adding a constant to every data point shifts the entire data set but does not change the spread. The mean changes by +2, but the deviations from the mean remain the same, so the standard deviation stays the same. Formally, if new data are $x_i' = x_i + c$ and new mean is $\mu' = \mu + c$, then $x_i' - \mu' = x_i - \mu$.
Question 6 EASY
In a survey of 100 students, 40 preferred coffee over tea. If 120 students are surveyed with the same preference rate, how many would be expected to prefer coffee?
  • A) 40
  • B) 48
  • C) 60
  • D) 72
Explanation:
Proportion preferring coffee = $\frac{40}{100} = 0.4$. Expected number in 120 = $0.4 \times 120 = 48$.

Question 8 MEDIUM
Two classes, Class A has 22 students with average score $78$, and Class B has 18 students with average score $85$. What is the overall average score of all 40 students?
  • A) $80$
  • B) $81.15$
  • C) $82$
  • D) $81.5$
Explanation:
Total score = $22\times 78 + 18\times 85 = 1716 + 1530 = 3246$. Overall average = $\frac{3246}{40} = 81.15$. Therefore, option B is correct. (A) would be the simple average of the two class means, which ignores class sizes; (C) and (D) are incorrect calculations.

Question 11 MEDIUM
During a 6-month period, the number of museum visitors rose from 2,500 to 3,000. (a) What is the percent increase? (b) If the same percentage increase occurs again during the next 6 months, what is the projected total number of visitors after 12 months?
  • A) $3,500$
  • B) $3,600$
  • C) $3,750$
  • D) $4,000$
Explanation:
(a) Percent increase = (3,000 − 2,500) / 2,500 = 500/2,500 = 0.20 = 20%. (b) If the same 20% increase occurs on 3,000, the next 6 months add 600, giving 3,000 × 1.20 = 3,600. Therefore, option B is correct. The other options reflect incorrect applications of the percent increase or noncompounding arithmetic.
Question 12 MEDIUM
Consider two data sets: A = {5, 7, 9, 11} and B = {5, 9, 9, 9}. Which data set has the larger standard deviation (spread)?
  • A) Data set A
  • B) Data set B
  • C) They have the same standard deviation
  • D) Not enough information
Explanation:
Compute the mean and deviations: Data set A has mean 8 and deviations ±3, ±1, ±1, ±3 with squared deviations summing to 20; variance = 20/4 = 5; stdev = sqrt(5) ≈ 2.236. Data set B has mean 8 and deviations −3, 1, 1, 1 with squared deviations summing to 12; variance = 12/4 = 3; stdev = sqrt(3) ≈ 1.732. Therefore, A has the larger standard deviation. The other options are false.

Question 14 MEDIUM
Scores: 60, 70, 75, 80, 85. If we add a new score of 100, which statistic increases more: the mean or the median?
  • A) The mean increases more than the median
  • B) The median increases more than the mean
  • C) Both increase by the same amount
  • D) The mean decreases while the median increases
Explanation:
Initial mean = (60+70+75+80+85)/5 = 370/5 = 74; median is 75. After adding 100, new mean = (470)/6 ≈ 78.33; new median is the average of the 3rd and 4th values in the ordered list: (75+80)/2 = 77.5. The mean increases by about 4.33, the median by 2.5, so the mean increases more. Therefore A is correct.
Question 15 MEDIUM
A bag contains 4 red, 3 blue, and 2 yellow marbles. Two marbles are drawn without replacement. What is the probability that at least one red marble is drawn?
  • A) $\frac{13}{18}$
  • B) $\frac{11}{18}$
  • C) $\frac{2}{3}$
  • D) $\frac{7}{9}$
Explanation:
Total ways to choose 2 marbles: $\binom{9}{2} = 36$. Ways with no red: choose 2 from the 5 non-red marbles (3 blue, 2 yellow): $\binom{5}{2} = 10$. So at least one red: $1 - \frac{10}{36} = \frac{26}{36} = \frac{13}{18}$. Therefore A is correct; others correspond to incorrect counts.
