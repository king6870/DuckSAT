Question 1 EASY
For the quadratic equation $3x^2 - 4x - 5 = 0$, the discriminant is $b^2 - 4ac$. How many real solutions does it have?
  • A) 0 real solutions
  • B) 1 real solution
  • C) 2 real solutions
  • D) infinitely many real solutions
Explanation:
Compute the discriminant: $b^2 - 4ac = (-4)^2 - 4(3)(-5) = 16 + 60 = 76$. Since $76 > 0$, the equation has two distinct real solutions. Therefore the correct choice is C.
Question 2 EASY
The parabola $y = -x^2 + 4x + 5$ can be rewritten in vertex form $y = -(x - h)^2 + k$. What is the vertex $(h,k)$ of the parabola?
  • A) (0,5)
  • B) (2,9)
  • C) (4,5)
  • D) (-2,9)
Explanation:
Complete the square: $y = -x^2 + 4x + 5 = -(x^2 - 4x) + 5 = -(x^2 - 4x + 4) + 5 + 4 = -(x - 2)^2 + 9$. The vertex is at $(h,k) = (2,9)$.
Question 3 MEDIUM
Factor the quadratic equation $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$ to find its roots.
  • A) $x = 2$ and $x = 3$
  • B) $x = -2$ and $x = -3$
  • C) $x = 1$ and $x = 6$
  • D) $x = -1$ and $x = -6$
Explanation:
Step 1: Factor $x^2 - 5x + 6$ as $(x-2)(x-3)$. Step 2: Set each factor equal to zero: $x-2=0$ or $x-3=0$. Step 3: Solve: $x=2$ or $x=3$. Thus the roots are $x=2$ and $x=3$.
Question 4 MEDIUM
Consider the quadratic $y = 2x^2 + 8x - 3$. Express it in vertex form and identify the vertex and axis of symmetry.
  • A) $y = 2(x+2)^2 - 11$, vertex $(-2, -11)$, axis of symmetry $x = -2$
  • B) $y = 2(x-2)^2 - 3$, vertex $(2, -3)$, axis of symmetry $x = 2$
  • C) $y = -2(x+2)^2 + 11$, vertex $(-2, 11)$, axis of symmetry $x = -2$
  • D) $y = 2(x+2)^2 + 3$, vertex $(-2, 3)$, axis of symmetry $x = -2$
Explanation:
Step 1: Start with $y=2x^2+8x-3$. Step 2: Factor out 2 from the x-terms: $y=2(x^2+4x)-3$. Step 3: Complete the square inside: $x^2+4x=(x+2)^2-4$. Step 4: Substitute: $y=2[(x+2)^2-4]-3=2(x+2)^2-11$. Thus vertex form is $y=2(x+2)^2-11$; vertex is $(-2,-11)$ and axis of symmetry is $x=-2$.
Question 5 MEDIUM
A projectile launched from a height of 5 feet with initial velocity 50 ft/s has height $y = -16t^2 + 50t + 5$. Using the discriminant, how many real solutions does $y = 0$ have?
  • A) Two real solutions, both positive
  • B) Two real solutions, one positive and one negative
  • C) One real solution
  • D) No real solutions
Explanation:
Step 1: Identify $a=-16$, $b=50$, $c=5$. Step 2: Compute the discriminant $\Delta=b^2-4ac=50^2-4(-16)(5)=2500+320=2820>0$, so there are two real solutions. Step 3: Solve $t=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{\Delta}}{2a}=\frac{-50\pm\sqrt{2820}}{-32}$. With $\sqrt{2820}\\approx53.14$, the roots are $t_1\approx\frac{-50+53.14}{-32}\approx-0.098$ and $t_2\approx\frac{-50-53.14}{-32}\\approx3.223$. Thus there are two real solutions, one negative and one positive.
