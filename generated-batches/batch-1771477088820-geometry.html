Question 1 EASY
In triangle $\triangle ABC$, with coordinates $A(0,0)$, $B(4,0)$, and $C(0,3)$, what is the area of triangle $\triangle ABC$?
  • A) 5
  • B) 6
  • C) 12
  • D) 9
Explanation:
Step 1: Identify base $\overline{AB}$ length. Since $A(0,0)$ and $B(4,0)$, $|\overline{AB}| = 4$. Step 2: Determine the height from $C$ to $\overline{AB}$. $\overline{AB}$ lies on the $x$-axis, and $C$ has $y$-coordinate 3, so the height is 3. Step 3: Apply the triangle area formula: $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times |\overline{AB}| \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 3 = 6$. Therefore, the area is $6$ square units.
Question 2 MEDIUM
In triangle $ riangle ABC$, $AB = 6$, $AC = 8$, and $\angle A = 90°$. The incircle radius $r$ of $\triangle ABC$ is what?
  • A) 1
  • B) 2
  • C) 3
  • D) 4
Explanation:
Let BC be the hypotenuse. By Pythagoras, $BC^2 = AB^2 + AC^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 100$, so $BC = 10$. The area $K$ of the right triangle is $K = \frac{1}{2} AB \cdot AC = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 8 = 24$. Semiperimeter $s = \frac{AB + BC + AC}{2} = \frac{6 + 10 + 8}{2} = 12$. The incircle radius is $r = \frac{K}{s} = \frac{24}{12} = 2$.
Question 3 MEDIUM
Let $A=(1,4)$ and $B=(7,-2)$ be two points. The line $AB$ has slope $m_{AB}$; Let $C=(4,2)$ be a point. What is the equation of the line through $C$ that is perpendicular to $AB$?
  • A) $y = x - 2$
  • B) $y = -x + 6$
  • C) $y = x + 2$
  • D) $y = -x - 2$
Explanation:
First compute the slope of AB: $m_{AB} = \frac{y_B - y_A}{x_B - x_A} = \frac{-2 - 4}{7 - 1} = \frac{-6}{6} = -1$. A line perpendicular to AB has slope $m_{\perp} = -\frac{1}{m_{AB}} = -\frac{1}{-1} = 1$. Through $C(4,2)$ with slope 1: $y - 2 = 1\cdot (x - 4)$, i.e. $y = x - 2$. So option A is correct.
