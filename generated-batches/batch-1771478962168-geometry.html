Question 1 MEDIUM
In the right triangle $\triangle ABC$ with right angle at $C$, $\angle A = 30^\circ$, $\angle B = 60^\circ$, and $\overline{AB} = 10$. Find $\overline{BC}$.
  • A) $\overline{BC} = 5$
  • B) $\overline{BC} = 5\sqrt{3}$
  • C) $\overline{BC} = 10$
  • D) $\overline{BC} = 5\sqrt{2}$
Explanation:
Step 1: A 30-60-90 triangle has hypotenuse = 2 × shorter leg. The side opposite $\angle A = 30^\circ$ is $\overline{BC}$, so $\overline{BC}$ is the shorter leg. Step 2: $\overline{AB} = 10$ is the hypotenuse, so $\overline{BC} = \frac{1}{2} \overline{AB} = \frac{1}{2}(10) = 5$. Therefore, $\overline{BC} = 5$.
Question 2 MEDIUM
In a circle with radius $4$, the arc subtended by a central angle of $60^\circ$ has length What is its length?
  • A) $\frac{4\pi}{3}$
  • B) $8\pi$
  • C) $\frac{2\pi}{3}$
  • D) $\frac{8\pi}{3}$
Explanation:
Arc length for central angle $\theta$ in degrees is $s = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \cdot 2\pi r$. Here $\theta=60^\circ$ and $r=4$. So $s = \frac{60}{360} \cdot 2\pi\cdot 4 = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 8\pi = \frac{4\pi}{3}$.

Question 4 MEDIUM
A composite shape consists of a rectangle $8\times 5$ with a right triangle of base $3$ and height $5$ attached to its left side. What is the area of the entire shape?
  • A) $\frac{95}{2}$
  • B) $40$
  • C) $43$
  • D) $52$
Explanation:
Rectangle area: $8\times 5 = 40$. Triangle area: $\tfrac{1}{2}\times 3\times 5 = \tfrac{15}{2} = 7.5$. Total area: $40 + 7.5 = 47.5 = \tfrac{95}{2}$.
Question 5 MEDIUM
Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. If the angle at the upper intersection on the left interior is $\angle A = 65^\circ$, what is $\angle B$, the interior angle on the lower intersection on the same side of the transversal?
  • A) $65^\circ$
  • B) $115^\circ$
  • C) $70^\circ$
  • D) $180^\circ$
Explanation:
Since the lines are parallel, the interior angles on the same side of the transversal are supplementary: $\angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ$. Thus $\angle B = 180^\circ - 65^\circ = 115^\circ$.

Question 10 MEDIUM
ΔABC ∼ ΔA'B'C' with corresponding sides AB ↔ A'B', BC ↔ B'C', AC ↔ A'C'. If $AB=6$, $BC=8$, and $A'B'=9$, find $B'C'$.
  • A) $12$
  • B) $10$
  • C) $14$
  • D) $16$
Explanation:
If the triangles are similar with corresponding sides, the scale factor is $k = \dfrac{A'B'}{AB} = \dfrac{9}{6} = \tfrac{3}{2}$. Then $B'C' = k\cdot BC = \tfrac{3}{2} \cdot 8 = 12$.
