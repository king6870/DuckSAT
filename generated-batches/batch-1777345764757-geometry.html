Question 68 HARD

  • B) 28
  • C) 40
  • D) 18
Explanation:
Use the shoelace formula: area $= \tfrac{1}{2}|x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2)|$. Let (x1,y1)=(1,2), (x2,y2)=(7,-4), (x3,y3)=(1,-10). Then: $1(-4 - (-10)) + 7((-10) - 2) + 1(2 - (-4)) = 1(6) + 7(-12) + 1(6) = 6 - 84 + 6 = -72$. Area $= \tfrac{1}{2}|-72| = 36$.
