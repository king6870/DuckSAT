In $\triangle ABC$, $\angle C = 90^\circ$, $\angle A = 30^\circ$, and the hypotenuse is $AB = 12$. What is the length of $BC$?
  • A) 6√3
  • B) 6
  • C) 12
  • D) 3
Explanation:
Since $\triangle ABC$ is a 30-60-90 triangle with the right angle at $C$, the side opposite $30^\circ$ (which is $BC$) is half the hypotenuse. Therefore $BC = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot AB = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 12 = 6$. The other leg would be $AC = 6\sqrt{3}$, and the hypotenuse remains $12$.

  • C) 6
  • D) 7

  • D) (-2,-5)

In triangle $\triangle ABC$, base $\overline{AB} = 8$ and the altitude from $C$ to $\overline{AB}$ has length 5. What is the area of $\triangle ABC$?
  • A) 40
  • B) 20
  • C) 15
  • D) 10
Explanation:
Area of a triangle is $\tfrac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 8 \cdot 5 = 20$.
Question 5 EASY
Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. The interior angles on the same side of the transversal are supplementary. If $m\angle A = 110^\circ$, what is $m\angle B$?
  • A) 60°
  • B) 70°
  • C) 110°
  • D) 90°
Explanation:
For interior angles on the same side, $m\angle A + m\angle B = 180^\circ$. Thus $m\angle B = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ$.
Question 6 EASY

  • A) (3,-4)
  • B) (3,4)
  • C) (-3,4)
  • D) (-3,-4)
Explanation:
Reflection across the $x$-axis changes the sign of the $y$-coordinate while keeping the $x$-coordinate the same. So $P' = (3, -(-4)) = (3, 4)$.

  • A) $y = -\frac{2}{3}x + 7$
  • B) $y = \frac{2}{3}x + 1$
  • C) $x = 3$
  • D) $y = -\frac{3}{2}x + 3$
Explanation:
Step 1: Slope of $PQ = (8-2)/(5-1) = 6/4 = 3/2$. Midpoint $M = ((1+5)/2, (2+8)/2) = (3,5)$. Step 2: Perpendicular slope is $-2/3$. Step 3: Through $M$, equation is $y-5 = (-2/3)(x-3)$, which simplifies to $y = -\frac{2}{3}x + 7$. Therefore, option A is correct.
