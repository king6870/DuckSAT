Question 1 MEDIUM
Solve for x: $2(3x - 4) + 5 = 3x + 1$.
  • A) $\frac{4}{3}$
  • B) $\frac{1}{3}$
  • C) 2
  • D) $-\frac{4}{3}$
Explanation:
Distribute: $2(3x - 4) = 6x - 8$. So the left side becomes $6x - 8 + 5 = 6x - 3$. The equation is $6x - 3 = 3x + 1$. Subtract $3x$ from both sides: $3x - 3 = 1$. Add 3: $3x = 4$. Divide by 3: $x = \frac{4}{3}$.

  • B) $x \in (-\infty, 1) \cup (6, \infty)$
  • C) $x \in [0, 5]$
  • D) $x \in [1, 5]$
Explanation:

  • D) x = 7, y = 1

A pencils-and-pens problem: Pencils cost 0.75 dollars and pens cost 1.25 dollars. If you buy 8 items for a total of 8.50 dollars, how many pencils did you buy?

  • B) 4 pencils and 4 pens
  • C) 5 pencils and 3 pens
  • D) 6 pencils and 2 pens
Explanation:
Let p be pencils and q be pens. Then p + q = 8 and 0.75p + 1.25q = 8.50. Substitute p = 8 - q: 0.75(8 - q) + 1.25q = 8.50 ⇒ 6 - 0.75q + 1.25q = 8.50 ⇒ 6 + 0.5q = 8.50 ⇒ 0.5q = 2.50 ⇒ q = 5, so p = 3.

Question 12 HARD
Solve the inequality $|2x - 3| > 5$. Which of the following describes the solution set?
  • A) $x < -1$ or $x > 4$
  • B) $-1 \le x \le 4$
  • C) $x \le -1$ or $x \ge 4$
  • D) -1 < x < 4
Explanation:
|2x - 3| > 5 splits into 2x - 3 > 5 or 2x - 3 < -5. The first gives 2x > 8 ⇒ x > 4. The second gives 2x < -2 ⇒ x < -1. Thus the solution is x < -1 or x > 4, i.e., (-∞, -1) ∪ (4, ∞).
Question 13 HARD
Two numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy the system of equations $x + y = 9$ and $2x - y = 1$. What is the value of $x$?
  • A) $\tfrac{10}{3}$
  • B) 3
  • C) $\tfrac{5}{2}$
  • D) 4
Explanation:
From x + y = 9, express y = 9 - x. Substitute into 2x - y = 1: 2x - (9 - x) = 1 ⇒ 2x - 9 + x = 1 ⇒ 3x = 10 ⇒ x = 10/3.
