Question 1 EASY
In triangle $\triangle ABC$, with $\angle A = 30^\circ$, $\angle B = 60^\circ$, $\angle C = 90^\circ$ and $AB = 6$, what is $BC$?
  • A) 3
  • B) 6
  • C) $3\sqrt{3}$
  • D) $6\sqrt{3}$
Explanation:
Since $\angle C = 90^\circ$, $AB$ is the hypotenuse. In a $30^\circ-60^\circ-90^\circ$ triangle, the sides opposite $30^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $90^\circ$ are in the ratio $1:\sqrt{3}:2$. Let the side opposite $30^\circ$ be $x$; then $2x = AB = 6$, so $x = 3$. The side $BC$ is opposite $\angle A = 30^\circ$, so $BC = x = 3$. Therefore $BC = 3$.
Question 2 EASY
In the coordinate plane, a circle has center $(2,-1)$ and radius $5$. Which of the following is the equation of the circle?
  • A) $$(x-2)^2 + (y+1)^2 = 25$$
  • B) $$(x+2)^2 + (y-1)^2 = 25$$
  • C) $$(x-2)^2 + (y+1)^2 = 10$$
  • D) $$(x-2)^2 + (y-1)^2 = 25$$
Explanation:
The circle with center $(h,k)$ and radius $r$ has equation $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$. Here $(h,k)=(2,-1)$ and $r=5$, so the equation is $(x-2)^2 + (y+1)^2 = 25$.
Question 3 EASY
In the coordinate plane, points $P(1,2)$ and $Q(4,6)$. The distance $d$ between $P$ and $Q$ is which of the following?
  • A) 5
  • B) $\sqrt{34}$
  • C) 3
  • D) $\sqrt{13}$
Explanation:
The distance formula gives $d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$. Here $(x_2-x_1)=4-1=3$ and $(y_2-y_1)=6-2=4$, so $d=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{9+16}=\sqrt{25}=5$.

Question 5 EASY
In a circle with radius $6$, the arc length corresponding to a central angle of $60^\circ$ is which of the following?
  • A) $2\pi$
  • B) $6\pi$
  • C) $4\pi$
  • D) $12\pi$
Explanation:
Arc length formula: $L=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\cdot 2\pi r$. For $\theta=60^\circ$, $r=6$: $L=\frac{60}{360}\cdot 2\pi\cdot 6=\frac{1}{6}\cdot 12\pi=2\pi$.
Question 6 EASY

  • B) (-4, 3)
  • C) (-3, -4)
  • D) (-4, -3)
Explanation:

In right triangle $\triangle ABC$, $\angle C = 90°$, $AB = 12$, and $\angle A = 30°$. What is the length of $\overline{BC}$?
  • A) 6
  • B) $6\sqrt{3}$
  • C) 12
  • D) $3\sqrt{3}$
Explanation:
Since $\angle C = 90°$, $AB$ is the hypotenuse. In a $30°-60°-90°$ triangle, the side opposite $30°$ is half the hypotenuse. Here $\angle A = 30°$, so $\overline{BC}$ (opposite $30°$) has length $AB/2 = 12/2 = 6$. For reference, the other leg is $AC = AB\cdot\sin 60° = 12\cdot(\sqrt{3}/2) = 6\sqrt{3}$.
Question 8 HARD
In coordinate geometry, let $A(1,2), B(5,8)$ and $C(-1,4)$. What is the area of $\triangle ABC$?
  • A) 10
  • B) 8
  • C) 12
  • D) 20
Explanation:
Compute vectors $\overrightarrow{AB}=(4,6)$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}=(-2,2)$. The area equals $\tfrac{1}{2}|\overrightarrow{AB}_x\cdot\overrightarrow{AC}_y-\overrightarrow{AB}_y\cdot\overrightarrow{AC}_x|=\tfrac{1}{2}|(4)(2)-(6)(-2)|=\tfrac{1}{2}(8+12)=10$.

In a circle with center $O$ and radius $6$, arc $AB$ subtends central angle $\angle AOB=120°$. What is the length of arc $AB$?

  • B) $6\pi$
  • C) $\tfrac{8\pi}{3}$
  • D) $12\pi$
Explanation:
Convert the angle to radians: $\theta=120°=\tfrac{2\pi}{3}$. Arc length is $L=r\theta=6\cdot\tfrac{2\pi}{3}=4\pi$.
Question 10 HARD
A regular hexagon has side length $s=4$. What is its area?
  • A) $24\sqrt{3}$
  • B) $12\sqrt{3}$
  • C) 48
  • D) $16\sqrt{3}$
Explanation:
A regular hexagon of side $s$ can be partitioned into 6 equilateral triangles of side $s$. Each triangle has area $\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}s^2$, so total area is $6\cdot\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}s^2=\tfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}s^2$. With $s=4$, area $=\tfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\cdot16=24\sqrt{3}$.
