Question 1 EASY
A population of bacteria starts at 200 and doubles every 3 hours. Which model correctly describes exponential growth of the population P as a function of time t (in hours)?
  • A) $P(t) = 200 + 10t$
  • B) $P(t) = 200 \cdot (1.2)^{t}$
  • C) $P(t) = 200 \cdot e^{t/3}$
  • D) $P(t) = 200 \cdot 2^{t/3}$
Explanation:
Step 1: Exponential growth has the form $P(t) = P_0 b^{t}$ with initial population $P_0 = 200$. Step 2: The population doubles every 3 hours, so $P(3) = 2P_0 = 400$. This implies $(b)^{3} = 2$, hence $b = 2^{1/3}$. Therefore $P(t) = 200(2^{1/3})^{t} = 200\cdot 2^{t/3}$. The option that matches this form is D; A and C are linear or use a different base, and B uses the wrong base per hour.

Step 3: Compare: 1469.3 > 1350, so exponential yields larger population after 5 years. Step 4: The difference is approximately $1469.3 - 1350 \approx 119.3$, i.e., about 119 people.
Question 10 HARD
Let $x$ satisfy $\\log_2(x^2 - 3x) - \\log_2 x = 3$, with domain $x>0$ and $x^2 - 3x > 0$. Solve for $x$.
  • A) 9
  • B) 12
  • C) 11
  • D) 8
Explanation:
Step 1: Use log properties: $\\log_2(x^2 - 3x) - \\log_2 x = \\log_2\left(\dfrac{x^2 - 3x}{x}\right) = \\log_2(x - 3)$. Step 2: Set equal to 3: $\\log_2(x - 3) = 3$; Step 3: Exponentiate: $x - 3 = 2^3 = 8$, so $x = 11$. Step 4: Check domain: $x > 0$ and $x^2 - 3x = 11^2 - 33 = 121 - 33 = 88 > 0$; thus valid. The solution is $x = 11$.
