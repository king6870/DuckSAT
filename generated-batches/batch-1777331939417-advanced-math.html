Question 1 EASY
Which expression best models a town's population after t years if the population grows by 4% per year and starts at 200 people?
  • A) $200 + 4t$
  • B) $200(1.04)^t$
  • C) $200(1.04)^{t+1}$
  • D) $200 + 0.04t$
Explanation:
Step 1: Identify the model. Exponential growth with initial population P0 = 200 and growth rate r = 0.04 is P(t) = P0(1 + r)^t = 200(1.04)^t. Step 2: Compare options: A) linear growth, D) linear growth with additive 0.04t, C) uses t+1 in the exponent, which incorrectly shifts the starting point, so incorrect. Therefore, option B is correct.
Question 2 EASY
Using properties of logarithms, evaluate $\\log_{3}(27) + \\log_{3}(9) - \\log_{3}(3)$. What is the value?
  • A) 3
  • B) 2
  • C) 4
  • D) 5
Explanation:
Step 1: Write each term using base-3 powers: $\\log_{3}(27)=\\log_{3}(3^3)=3$, $\\log_{3}(9)=\\log_{3}(3^2)=2$, $\\log_{3}(3)=\\log_{3}(3^1)=1$. Step 2: Combine: $3 + 2 - 1 = 4$. Therefore, the value is 4.
Question 3 MEDIUM
An city population grows exponentially at a rate of 2.5% per year. If the initial population is $120000$, the model is $P(t) = 120000(1.025)^t$, where t is in years. About how many years will it take for the population to reach 150000? (Choose the closest integer.)
  • A) 8 years
  • B) 9 years
  • C) 10 years
  • D) 12 years
Explanation:
Step 1: Set up the equation using the exponential model: $120000(1.025)^t = 150000$. Step 2: Divide both sides by 120000: $(1.025)^t = \frac{150000}{120000} = 1.25$. Step 3: Take natural logs: $t\ln(1.025) = \ln(1.25)$. Step 4: Solve for t: $t = \dfrac{\ln(1.25)}{\ln(1.025)}$. Step 5: Approximate: $\ln(1.25) \approx 0.2231436$ and $\ln(1.025) \approx 0.0246926$, so $t \approx 9.04$, i.e., about 9 years. Note: if growth were linear with a fixed annual increase of 2.5% of the initial population, the model would be $P(t) \approx 120000 + 0.025 \cdot 120000 \cdot t = 120000 + 3000t$, which would reach 150000 after 10 years, illustrating the difference between exponential and linear growth.
Question 4 MEDIUM
Let $\\log_{2}$ denote the base-2 logarithm. If $\\log_{2}(2x) = \\log_{2}(5x - 3)$ with the domain constraint $x > \frac{3}{5}$, solve for $x$.
  • A) 0
  • B) 1
  • C) $\tfrac{3}{5}$
  • D) 2
Explanation:
Since the logarithms have the same base and are equal, their arguments must be equal: $2x = 5x - 3$. Solve: $-3x = -3 \Rightarrow x = 1$. The domain requires $x > \frac{3}{5}$, which is satisfied. Therefore, $x = 1$ is the solution.
Question 5 MEDIUM
Compute the quotient and express in the form $a + bi$ where $i^2 = -1$: $\dfrac{3 - 4i}{1 + i}$.
  • A) $1 - 7i$
  • B) $-\tfrac{1}{2} - \tfrac{7}{2}i$
  • C) $-2 + i$
  • D) $2 - 3i$
Explanation:
To divide by a complex number, multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator: $\dfrac{3 - 4i}{1 + i} \cdot \dfrac{1 - i}{1 - i} = \dfrac{(3 - 4i)(1 - i)}{(1 + i)(1 - i)}$. Compute the numerator: $(3 - 4i)(1 - i) = 3 - 3i - 4i + 4i^2 = 3 - 7i - 4 = -1 - 7i$. Denominator: $(1 + i)(1 - i) = 1 - i^2 = 2$. Therefore, the result is $\dfrac{-1 - 7i}{2} = -\tfrac{1}{2} - \tfrac{7}{2}i$.
