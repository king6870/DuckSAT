  • A) 1120

  • C) 1217

Explanation:

Explanation:

Step 3: Add numerators: $3x(x+1) + 2(x-1) = 3x^2+3x+2x-2 = 3x^2+5x-2$. Therefore the sum is $\frac{3x^2+5x-2}{x^2-1}$. The domain restrictions are $x eq 1, -1$.

Question 2 (Logarithms): If $\log_2 (3x+1) - \log_2 (x-1) = 3$, what is x?
  • A) $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$
  • B) $x = 2$
  • C) $x = \dfrac{9}{5}$
  • D) $x = \dfrac{4}{3}$
Explanation:

Step 3: Solve: $3x+1 = 8(x-1) = 8x - 8 \Rightarrow 9 = 5x \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{9}{5}$. Step 4: Check domain: require $x-1>0 \Rightarrow x>1$, and $9/5>1$, so valid. The correct answer is $\dfrac{9}{5}$.

Question 3 (Rational expressions): Simplify the sum $\frac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 - x} + \frac{2x - 2}{x - 1}$ and state all restrictions on x.
  • A) $\dfrac{3x+1}{x}$, with $x eq 0,1$
  • B) $\dfrac{3x+1}{x-1}$, with $x eq 1$
  • C) $\dfrac{3x+1}{x+1}$, with $x eq -1,0$
  • D) $\dfrac{3x+2}{x}$, with $x eq 0,1$
Explanation:
Step 1: Factor: $x^2 - 1 = (x-1)(x+1)$ and $x^2 - x = x(x-1)$. So the first term becomes $\frac{(x-1)(x+1)}{x(x-1)} = \frac{x+1}{x}$ for $x eq 0,1$. Step 2: The second term $\frac{2x-2}{x-1} = \frac{2(x-1)}{x-1} = 2$ for $x eq 1$. Step 3: Sum: $\frac{x+1}{x} + 2 = \frac{x+1}{x} + \frac{2x}{x} = \frac{3x+1}{x}$. Restrictions come from the original denominators: $x eq 0$ and $x eq 1$. The simplified form is $\dfrac{3x+1}{x}$ with those restrictions.
