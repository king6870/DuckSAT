Question 1 EASY
In right triangle $\triangle ABC$ with a right angle at $C$, $AB = 5$ and $AC = 3$. What is the length of $BC$?
  • A) 4
  • B) 2
  • C) $\sqrt{34}$
  • D) $\sqrt{6}$
Explanation:
Use the Pythagorean theorem in the right triangle: $AB^2 = AC^2 + BC^2$. Substituting gives $5^2 = 3^2 + BC^2$, so $25 = 9 + BC^2$ and $BC^2 = 16$. Therefore $BC = 4$.
Question 2 EASY
In coordinate geometry, what is the distance between $P(1,2)$ and $Q(4,6)$?
  • A) 3
  • B) 5
  • C) $\sqrt{5}$
  • D) $\sqrt{13}$
Explanation:
Distance formula: $d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$. Here $x_2-x_1 = 4-1 = 3$ and $y_2-y_1 = 6-2 = 4$. Thus $d = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9+16} = \sqrt{25} = 5$.
Question 3 EASY
The circle with center $(2,-3)$ passes through $(5,-3)$. Its equation in the form $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$ is which of the following?
  • A) $(x-2)^2 + (y+3)^2 = 9$
  • B) $(x-2)^2 + (y+3)^2 = 6$
  • C) $(x+2)^2 + (y+3)^2 = 9$
  • D) $(x-2)^2 + (y-3)^2 = 9$
Explanation:
The radius is the distance from the center to the given point: $r = \sqrt{(5-2)^2 + (-3+3)^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + 0^2} = 3$. With center $(h,k)=(2,-3)$, the equation is $(x-2)^2 + (y+3)^2 = 3^2 = 9$.

A composite figure consists of a $6\text{ by }4$ rectangle and a right triangle with base 2 and height 4 attached to the top edge. What is the total area?
  • A) 28
  • B) 24
  • C) 30
  • D) 32
Explanation:
Rectangle area: $6 \times 4 = 24$. Triangle area: $\tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 4 = 4$. Total area: $24 + 4 = 28$.
Question 5 EASY
Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. If interior angles on the same side of the transversal satisfy $m\angle A = 60^\circ$, what is $m\angle B$ when $\angle B$ is on the same side?
  • A) 60
  • B) 120
  • C) 30
  • D) 90
Explanation:
Interior angles on the same side of the transversal are supplementary: $m\angle B = 180^\circ - m\angle A = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ$.

Question 7 HARD
In triangle $\triangle ABC$, AB = 6, AC = 8, BC = 10. The altitude from A to BC meets BC at D. What is the length of AD?
  • A) $\dfrac{24}{5}$
  • B) $6$
  • C) $4$
  • D) $\dfrac{12}{5}$
Explanation:
Step 1: Since $AB^2 + AC^2 = BC^2$ (i.e., $6^2 + 8^2 = 10^2$), angle $A$ is a right angle. Step 2: The altitude from the right angle to the hypotenuse has length $AD = \dfrac{AB \cdot AC}{BC} = \dfrac{6 \cdot 8}{10} = \dfrac{48}{10} = \dfrac{24}{5}$. Step 3: Therefore $AD = \dfrac{24}{5}$.
Question 8 HARD
In circle with center O and radius $R=5$, chord AB has length AB = 6. Find the measure of the central angle $\angle AOB$ (in degrees).
  • A) $60°$
  • B) $73.74°$
  • C) $106.26°$
  • D) $120°$
Explanation:
Let $\theta = \angle AOB$. For a chord, $AB = 2R \sin(\tfrac{\theta}{2})$. With $AB=6$ and $R=5$, $6 = 2\cdot 5 \sin(\tfrac{\theta}{2})$, so $\sin(\tfrac{\theta}{2}) = \tfrac{3}{5}$. Thus $\tfrac{\theta}{2} = \arcsin(\tfrac{3}{5}) \approx 36.8699°$, giving $\theta \approx 73.7398°$, i.e., $73.74°$.
Question 9 HARD
In the coordinate plane, let $A=(1,2)$ and $B=(7,-4)$. Point $C$ lies on line $AB$ such that $AC:CB=1:2$. Find the coordinates of $C$.
  • A) $(3,0)$
  • B) $(5,-2)$
  • C) $(7,-4)$
  • D) $(3,-1)$
Explanation:
If $AC:CB=1:2$, then $C$ divides segment $AB$ internally in the ratio $1:2$. By the section formula, $C = \left(\dfrac{2x_A+x_B}{3}, \dfrac{2y_A+y_B}{3}\right)$. With $A=(1,2)$ and $B=(7,-4)$, $C = \left(\dfrac{2(1)+7}{3}, \dfrac{2(2)+(-4)}{3}\right) = (3,0)$.
Question 10 HARD
A regular hexagon with side length 6 is inscribed in a circle. Find the difference between the circle's area and the hexagon's area.
  • A) $36\pi - 54\sqrt{3}$
  • B) $36\pi - 54$
  • C) $54\sqrt{3} - 36\pi$
  • D) $36\pi - 54\sqrt{2}$
Explanation:
For a regular hexagon with side length $s$, the circumradius equals $R=s$, so with $s=6$ we have $R=6$. Circle area is $\pi R^2 = 36\pi$. The hexagon area is $A_{hex} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}s^2 = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\cdot 36 = 54\sqrt{3}$. The difference is $36\pi - 54\sqrt{3}$.
